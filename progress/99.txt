## Findings from The "Hard Science" Audit (Tracks 311-314)
1.  **Imperative Fail:** Verbs (`qokeey`) do NOT start sentences in recipes. They sit in Position 3.
2.  **Morphology Win:** The language is highly inflected (33% compression). Roots like `qok` take 14+ suffixes.
3.  **Positional Rigidity:** Words like `sho` (Fire) and `saiin` (Water/Star) are rigidly locked to the **Start** of lines. Words like `sal` (Salt) are locked to the **End**.

## The New Hypothesis: "Particle-Topic-Verb-Object"
The sentence structure seems to be:
`[Particle/Starter] [Topic/Subject] [Verb] [Object]`
*   **Starter:** `sho`, `saiin` (Markers of context? "Regarding Fire...")
*   **Topic:** The main noun being discussed.
*   **Verb:** `qokeey` (The action).
*   **Object:** `sal` (The ingredient).
