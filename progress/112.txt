## The Codebook Theory
The manuscript is not a phonetic script. It is a **Codebook**.
Words are arbitrary symbols assigned to concepts (`opchy` = Cannabis).
The grammar is the key to reading the relationships between these concepts.

## The Strategy (Iter 112)
1.  **Semantic Classes (Track 356):** Group nouns by their context (what adjectives modify them?).
2.  **Grammar Rules (Track 357):** Define the role of Function Words (`daiin` = Start?).
3.  **Visual Mapping (Track 358):** Link Local Variables to Section Features (Stars, Nymphs).
