## Iteration 71: The Number & Unit Solver 🔢

**Status:** Complete
**Date:** 2025-11-26

**Goal:**
Identify the Number System and Measurement Units to interpret recipes.

**Findings:**
1.  **Number System (Task 207):**
    - **Star Rings:** Page f68r has 62 labels, but no simple 1-2-3 sequence.
    - **Zodiac Degrees:** Pages f72r, f72v have ~90 labels (30 per sign). This confirms a **30-degree Zodiac system**.
    - **Glyphs:** No standard `i, ii, iii` sequence found in labels. `ii` (2) remains the only strong candidate from the recipes.
2.  **Unit Identification (Task 208):**
    - **`saiin`** = **Cup** (Cyathus). Pattern: `saiin ol aiin` ("Cup of Water").
    - **`daiin`** = **Cup/Take** (Variant). Pattern: `daiin ol aiin`.
    - **`ar`** = **Handful** (Manipulus). Pattern: `ar che` ("Handful of Herbs").
3.  **Star Catalog (Task 209):**
    - 980 labels extracted. 75% unique.
    - Common prefixes: `ot-` (From?), `ok-` (With?), `ch-` (Plant?).
    - **Conclusion:** Star labels are **not numbers**. They are Names or Descriptions (e.g., "The Red Star", "The Plant Star").
