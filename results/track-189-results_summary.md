# Track 189 Results Summary

## Findings
Analysis of prefixes `ot-`, `ok-`, `or-`, `op-`, `ol-` and their preceding verbs reveals distinct patterns correlating with the verb `daiin` (Take/From Spring).

### Proposed Case Mapping
Based on the high frequency of association with `daiin`:

1.  **ok- (Accusative):** Most strongly correlated with `daiin` (105 instances). Likely marks the **Direct Object** (the item being taken).
2.  **ot- (Ablative):** Strong correlation (71 instances). Likely marks the **Source** (taken *from*).
3.  **ol- (Dative):** Moderate correlation (38 instances). Likely marks the **Destination** (taken *to*).
4.  **or- (Locative):** Lower correlation with action verbs, potentially marking **Location** (at/in).
5.  **op- (Instrumental):** Lowest correlation with `daiin`, potentially marking **Means** (with/by).
