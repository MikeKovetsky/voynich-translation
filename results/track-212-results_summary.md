Search for visual evidence (drawings of cups, hands) corresponding to the proposed measurement units `saiin` (Cup) and `ar` (Handful) in Voynich Quire 20 (f103r-f116v).

## Key Insights
1.  **The "Star" Correlation**: The marginal stars in Quire 20 likely function as bullet points. `saiin` (appearing at the start of these bulleted sections) is the text equivalent of the bullet—functioning as "Item", "Take", or "Cup of".
2.  **Abstraction**: The lack of literal illustrations in Quire 20 (unlike the Pharma section) suggests this section relies on standardized text units (`saiin`) rather than pictorial aids for measurements.
