# Track 235: The Aiin Investigation - Results Summary

## 1. Key Findings
- **Distribution**: `aiin` is overwhelmingly a "Dry" word.
  - **Dry Pages**: 273 instances.
  - **Wet Pages**: Only 13 instances.
  - This stark contrast (21:1 ratio) contradicts the previous "Spring/Water" definition.
- **Star Connection**:
  - High frequency in **f104r** (27 instances) and **f104v** (41 instances), which are "text-only" pages but noted for "red/yellow stars" in their headers.
  - Collocation with `os` ("Star/Blue").
  - **Hebrew Connection**: `Ayin` (Eye) maps well to "Star" (Eye of the Night).
- **Morphology**:
  - `daiin` is also predominantly Dry (301 vs 86) but has a wider usage than `aiin`.
  - `oaiin` (12 vs 0) is exclusively Dry.

## 2. Hypothesis Evaluation
1. **Hebrew `Ayin` (Eye/Star)**: **Strongly Supported**.
   - Distribution matches "Dry/Fire/Star" pages.
   - "Eye" is a common metaphor for stars.
   - Visual similarity: `aiin` looks like "eyes" or digits.
2. **Hebrew `Yayin` (Wine)**: **Weak**.
   - "Dry" distribution works for "Red Wine" (Mars), but lack of "Wet" usage is odd for a liquid.
   - Collocation with `chedy` (Mix) is the only support, but "Mixing Stars" (or "Aligning Stars") is also possible.
3. **Arabic `Ain` (Essence/Substance)**: **Plausible**.
   - As a counterpart to `ol` (Water/Liquid), `aiin` could be "Substance/Solid".
   - But the specific "Star" page density points more to "Star/Aether".

## 3. Recommendation
- **Redefine `aiin`**: Change meaning from "Spring/Source" to **"Star"** or **"Eye (of Heaven)"**.
- **Category**: Move from "Element/Water" to "Cosmos/Fire".
- **Collocations**: Investigate `daiin` as "The Star" or "Of the Star".
