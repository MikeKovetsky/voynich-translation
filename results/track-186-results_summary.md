# Track 186 Results Summary

## Goal
Re-evaluate `o-` prefixed words as Case Markers or Prepositions.

## Key Findings
- Most frequent prefix: `ok-` (2374 occurrences).
- Prefixes appearing frequently at start of phrases (>25%): `ot-`, `ok-`, `or-`, `op-`, `ol-`.
- Contrast pairs identified: 807 total pairs.

## Recommendation
- `ol-` is a strong candidate for a sentence-initial marker (Definite/Nominative).

See `results/grammar_cases.md` for full details.
