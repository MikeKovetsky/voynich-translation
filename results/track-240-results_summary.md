# Track 240 Results Summary: Rosettes Translation

## Overview
This task aimed to translate the text of the Rosettes Foldout (f86v) to validate the "Flow of Influence" theory, specifically looking for a progression from Stars (`aiin`) to Earth (`o`) to Water (`ol`).

## Methodology
- Extracted text from `data/eva_ivtff.txt` for folio `f86v`.
- Sections analyzed: f86v.3, f86v.4, f86v.5, f86v.6.
- Translated using Dictionary v10.0.
- Counted occurrences of Elemental Keywords:
  - **Star**: `aiin`, `daiin`
  - **Earth**: `o`, `oteos`
  - **Water**: `ol`, `okeedy`

## Key Findings

### 1. Elemental Distribution
| Section | Star (aiin) | Earth (o) | Water (ol) | Ratio (Water/Star) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **f86v.3** | 12 | 0 | 1 | 0.08 |
| **f86v.4** | 10 | 1 | 7 | 0.70 |
| **f86v.5** | 21 | 0 | 7 | 0.33 |
| **f86v.6** | 12 | 0 | 8 | 0.67 |

### 2. Flow of Influence
- **Star Dominance**: All analyzed sections are dominated by "Star" terminology (`aiin`/`daiin`). This suggests the entire Rosettes map is primarily a "Celestial" or "Stellar" diagram, or describes the influence of stars on the other elements.
- **Water Emergence**: 
  - Section **f86v.3** is almost exclusively "Star" (12:1).
  - Sections **f86v.4** and **f86v.6** show a strong "Water" presence (ratios of 0.70 and 0.67), suggesting these areas represent the "mixing" or "delivery" of influence into the liquid medium (tubes/pipes).
- **Missing Earth**: "Earth" words (`o`/`oteos`) are virtually absent. This implies the map might depict the *source* (Stars) and the *medium* (Water) rather than the *destination* (Earth), or that "Earth" is represented by different vocabulary in this context.

## Conclusion
The translation supports a "Flow" theory where "Star" influence is the primary source. There is a distinct differentiation between sections:
- **f86v.3**: Pure Source (Star).
- **f86v.4 & f86v.6**: Transmission/Mixing Zones (High Water content).
- **f86v.5**: Intermediate Zone (High Star, Moderate Water).

The "tubes" and connections in the Rosettes likely represent the flow of this stellar influence (`aiin`) through the water/liquid medium (`ol`) to the rest of the system.
