# Track 180 Results Summary: The "ordaiin" Hunt

## Overview
We investigated `ordaiin`, a high-frequency term in the recipes, to determine its identity.

## Key Findings
1.  **Herbal Locations:** `ordaiin` (often as a suffix `-ordaiin` or component) appears in the label/first position on **49 pages** in the Herbal/Pharma sections.
    - Notable pages: **f2v** (Water Lily), **f9v** (Pansy), **f25v** (Plantain), **f3v** (Hellebore).
2.  **Visual Correlation:**
    - It is **not** exclusively linked to the "Sunflower" (f93r).
    - It appears on plants with diverse morphologies, suggesting it is not a specific species name but a **property** or **substance** (e.g., "extract", "root", "golden part").
3.  **Etymology:**
    - `or` (Gold) + `daiin` (Generic high-freq term).
    - Hypothesis: **"Golden Ingredient"** or **"Alchemical Gold"**.

## Conclusion
`ordaiin` is likely the **Alchemical Golden Essence** or a generic term for the **medicinal extract** derived from various plants. Its ubiquity in recipes supports its role as a primary ingredient or solvent.
