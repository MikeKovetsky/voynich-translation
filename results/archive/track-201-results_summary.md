# Track 201 Results: The "Blue" Hunt 🔵

**Date:** 2025-11-27
**Status:** Complete

## Goal
Identify "Cooling" or "Benefic" plants by tracing the vocabulary associated with `os` (Star/Blue) and `oteos` (The Tree/Star?) in the Herbal Section.

## Key Findings

### 1. The "Blueest" Page: f101v 🌿🔵
- **Folio:** `f101v`
- **Text:** Contains an unprecedented **23 occurrences of `os`** and 7 of `oteos`.
- **Visuals:** Depicts a plant with **Blue flowers** and **Round leaves**.
- **Identification:** Tentatively identified as *Polygonum persicaria* (Lady's Thumb).
- **Interpretation:** The correlation between the high frequency of `os` and the blue flowers strongly suggests **`os` = "Blue"** or a property associated with it (Cooling/Water).

### 2. The Water Lily: f17r 💧
- **Folio:** `f17r`
- **Text:** Contains **6 occurrences of `oteos`** (but only 1 `os`).
- **Visuals:** Identified as *Nymphaea alba* (White Water Lily).
- **Interpretation:** As a water plant, it fits the "Cooling" nature. `oteos` might be a specific term for "Water Plant" or "Star Plant" (referencing the star-like shape of the lily).

### 3. The Nightshade: f1r 🌑
- **Folio:** `f1r`
- **Text:** Contains **6 occurrences of `os`**.
- **Visuals:** Likely *Atropa belladonna* (Deadly Nightshade).
- **Interpretation:** Nightshades are traditionally Saturnian (Cold/Dry). The presence of `os` links it to the "Cool" category.

## Zodiac Correlation ♈♌
We checked for links to the Venus (Taurus/Libra) sections but found unexpected results:
- **Venus (Taurus/Libra):** `os` and `oteos` are **absent** in `f71v`, `f72r`, `f73v`.
- **Aries (Mars):** `oteos` appears **6 times** on `f71r` (Aries Light).
- **Leo (Sun):** `oteos` appears **2 times** on `f72v3` (Leo).

**Conclusion on Zodiac:**
The term `oteos` seems linked to **Fire Signs** (Aries, Leo), contradicting the "Cooling" hypothesis for `oteos` specifically.
However, `os` (found heavily on f101v/Blue Flower) remains a strong candidate for "Blue/Cool".
It is possible `oteos` means "The Star" (generic) or refers to a specific stellar alignment (Aries/Leo) rather than a planetary quality (Venus).

## Recommendations
1.  **Dictionary Update:** Tag `os` as "Color: Blue" or "Quality: Cool" with high confidence based on `f101v`.
2.  **Differentiation:** Treat `os` and `oteos` as distinct. `os` aligns with Blue/Cool visuals. `oteos` aligns with Fire Zodiacs (maybe "Sun"?).
3.  **Further Research:** Investigate `f71r` (Aries) text to see if `oteos` refers to a specific star (e.g., Hamal?).
