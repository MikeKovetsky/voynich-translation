## Proposed Mapping

Based on the high correlation with `daiin` (meaning "to take" or "from the spring"):

| Prefix | Correlation | Proposed Case | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ok-** | Strongest with `daiin` (105) | **Accusative** | Direct Object (The item taken) |
| **ot-** | Strong with `daiin` (71) | **Ablative** | Source (Taken *from*) |
| **ol-** | Moderate with `daiin` (38) | **Dative** | Destination (Taken *to*) |
| **or-** | Low with `daiin` | **Locative** | Location (At/In) |
| **op-** | Low with `daiin` | **Instrumental** | Means (With/By) |
