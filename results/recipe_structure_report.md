### Interpretation of Amount Markers
The words `shey`, `sheey`, `shek` appear frequently immediately after `daiin` or between `daiin` and another noun.
- Pattern: `daiin` + [Possible Amount/Action] + [Ingredient]
- Examples: `daiin shek ...`, `daiin shey ...`
- `shey` might function as a particle ("of") or a measure ("one").
- `ol` and `al` also appear in this slot (`daiin ol ...`, `daiin al ...`).

### Interpretation of Process Verbs
- `chedy` (related to herbs/leaves) appears at the end, possibly "process the herbs" or "add herbs".
- `qokeey` (related to water/liquid `o-` + `key`) might mean "drink" or "boil".
- Words ending in `-am` (`dam`, `alkam`) appear at the end, a common suffix for imperative or future tense in other sections. `dam` could be "drink".
