## Observations
- The text starts with a Verb (`koaiin`), which is unusual for SVO but common in VSO languages or imperative lists.
- Line 14 contains a cluster of proper nouns/names: TOL, EAIN.
- Line 15 starts with `daiin` ("Then"), followed by a Verb (`taedaiin`). This strongly supports a narrative structure ("Then [Subject] Verbed...").
- The "Verb" count is low (only ~12% of words), suggesting either a very descriptive text (lots of nouns/adjectives) or the `-a/-n` suffix rule is incomplete.
