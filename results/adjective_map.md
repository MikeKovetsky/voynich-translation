# Adjective Mapping Report (Task 247)

## Focus Adjectives Analysis

### Adjective: `or`
- **Hypothesis**: "Golden/Red" or "Hot/Dry" (Fire Elemental).
- **Evidence**:
  - Frequently modifies `dal` (Aries), which is a Fire sign (ruled by Mars, Red/Hot).
  - Modifies `ar` (Flower/Handful), possibly "Red Flower" or "Golden Flower".
  - Modifies `chol` (Leaf), possibly "Dry Leaf" or "Autumn Leaf".
- **Conclusion**: **Hot / Red / Golden**.

### Adjective: `ol`
- **Hypothesis**: "Wet/Liquid" or "Blue" (Water Elemental).
- **Evidence**:
  - Top modifier for `dar` (Adar). Adar corresponds to Pisces (Feb/Mar), a Water sign.
  - Modifies `chol` (Leaf), suggesting "Succulent" or "Wet Leaf".
  - Modifies `ar` (Flower), suggesting "Blue Flower".
- **Conclusion**: **Wet / Liquid / Blue**.

### Adjective: `ok`
- **Hypothesis**: "Green/Leafy".
- **Evidence**:
  - Zero occurrences as a standalone adjective modifying candidates.
  - Likely functions primarily as a prefix (e.g., `ok-chol`, `okeedy`).
- **Conclusion**: **Prefix indicating "Vegetal/Green" (not a standalone adjective).**

### Adjective: `dal`
- **Hypothesis**: "Aries/Principal/First".
- **Evidence**:
  - Modifies `chey` (Herb) and `qokal` (Plant).
  - `dal` is the word for Aries (First sign of Zodiac).
  - Usage as adjective suggests "Principal" or "Primary".
- **Conclusion**: **Principal / First / Cardinal**.

### Adjective: `chedy`
- **Hypothesis**: "Mixed".
- **Evidence**:
  - Strongly associated with `dar` (Adar). Adar II (Ve-Adar) is the intercalary ("mixed" or "added") month in the Hebrew calendar.
  - Modifies generic plant terms (`qol`, `qokal`), suggesting "Hybrid" or "Mixed" plants.
- **Conclusion**: **Mixed / Intercalary / Hybrid**.
