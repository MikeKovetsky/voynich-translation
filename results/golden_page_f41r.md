## Footnotes
1.  **pchey / shekchey** (*Thistle*): Identified as Thistle based on visual grounding and morphological analysis (`shek` = thorn/spine).
2.  **oked / oteey** (*The Green*): `oteey` relates to "leaf" or "foliage" (green parts). `ok-` is the object marker.
3.  **qotcheedy** (*Prepare the Thistle*): `qo-` (Verbalizer) + `tcheedy` (Thistle variant) + `-y` (Imperative). "Make the Thistle [mixture]".
4.  **qotchy sal** (*Pinch the Salt*): `qotchy` (Finger/Pinch) + `sal` (Salt). A clear instruction found in multiple recipes. `sal` is a high-confidence cognate.
5.  **shok** (*Leg*): Hebrew cognate `shok` (leg/thigh). Suggests the poultice is applied to the leg.
6.  **chekeedy** (*Scabiosa*): *Scabiosa* plants were often used for skin conditions ("scab").
