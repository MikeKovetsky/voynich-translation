## Analysis & Logic Check

### Verb Patterns
The verb `qokeey` (DRINK/BOIL) appears frequently in these recipes, often towards the end of a sequence or after ingredients are listed.
- **Recipe #4**: `qokeey` appears in line f103v.12 ("DRINK/BOIL TO-the/of WHICH/THAT FLOWER").
- **Recipe #10**: `qokeey` appears in line f104r.44 ("DRINK/BOIL TO/AT SICK").
- **Recipe #32**: `qokeey` appears multiple times (f111v.5, f111v.6, f111v.9), suggesting a process of boiling or drinking repeatedly.

### Recipe Logic (`daiin` ... `qokeey`)
The structure `daiin` [Ingredients] ... `qokeey` [Action] holds up well across the selected samples.
- **Start**: All selected recipes start with `daiin` (TAKE/FROM), followed immediately by a noun/ingredient (e.g., `char` "ROOT", `shey` "AMOUNT", `chol` "LEAF").
- **Middle**: Lists of plants (`chol`, `char`, `sain`, `sar`) and amounts (`shey`).
- **End/Action**: Instructions to `qokeey` (DRINK/BOIL) or apply the mixture (`okam` "PREPARATION/MIXTURE").

### Key Vocabulary Usage
- **`chol` (Leaf)**: Used in context with specific plants, e.g., `chol chedy` (Leaf of Herb).
- **`char` (Root)**: Often the first ingredient taken (`daiin char` - Take Root).
- **`aiin` (Spring/Source)**: Very frequent, possibly indicating water ("Spring water") is the base liquid for these decoctions.
