# Task 172: The `ed` Root Investigation

## Goal
Determine the meaning of the `ed` root (and its variants `eda`, `edy`, `o-ed`). It is a massive cluster center and appears in both "Topic 1" (Astro) and "Topic 4" (Recipes).

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json`
- `results/noun_clusters.json`

## Process
1.  **Concordance:** Find all sentences containing `ed` (as root).
    - *Note:* Be careful with segmentation. Look for `ed`, `o-ed`, `qok-ed`, `y-ed`.
2.  **Collocation Analysis:**
    - What verbs act on `ed`? (Is it "eaten"? "seen"? "mixed"?)
    - What adjectives describe `ed`?
3.  **Morphological Analysis:**
    - Does `ed` take the `o-` (Noun) prefix?
    - Does it take the `qok-` (Verb) prefix?
    - Does it take the `dy` (Suffix)?
4.  **Hypothesis Testing:**
    - Check if `ed` fits: "Light", "Fire", "Air", "Mix", "Stone".

## Output
- `results/root_ed_analysis.md`: Detailed linguistic profile of `ed`.
- `results/track-172-results_summary.md`: Summary of findings.
