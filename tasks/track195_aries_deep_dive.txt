# Task 195: Aries Deep Dive

## Goal
Analyze the "Aries" section (f70r-f71r) to understand how Nettle (`choly`) and Thistle (`ald`) are described. This validates the "Mars" connection.

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (f70r-f71r)
- `results/dictionary/dictionary.json`

## Process
1.  **Locate Sentences:** Find every sentence containing `ald` or `choly` (or variations).
2.  **Adjective Analysis:**
    - List all modifiers (`o-[Root]`) attached to these words.
    - Translate the modifiers. Do they mean "Sharp", "Hot", "Red"?
3.  **Context:**
    - What verbs are used? "Cut"? "Burn"? "Take"?

## Output
- `results/aries_analysis.md`: Detailed report on the Nettle/Thistle passages.
- `results/track-195-results_summary.md`: Summary.
