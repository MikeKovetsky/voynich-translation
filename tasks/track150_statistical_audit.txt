Task: Statistical Audit of Translation v7.4

We need to scientifically validate our current "Best Effort" translation. It feels too much like a "keyword list" and not enough like a natural language.

Input Files:
- /translated/*.md (The current translation output)
- data/voynich_transcription.txt (The raw source text)

Subtasks:
1. **Entropy Analysis:**
   - Calculate the 2nd-order entropy of the raw Voynich text.
   - Calculate the 2nd-order entropy of our English translation.
   - Compare them. If Voynich is 2.5 bits/char and our English is 4.5 bits/char, we are "hallucinating" information. They should be roughly similar (or English should be slightly lower if Voynich is verbose).

2. **Zipf's Law Check:**
   - Plot the frequency rank vs. frequency for the translated English words. Does it follow a power law (straight line on log-log graph)?
   - If our translation is just repeating "Leaf" and "Mix" too often, the curve will be distorted.

3. **Repetition Analysis:**
   - Voynich is known for immediate repetition (`chol chol`).
   - Check our translation. Do we translate `chol chol` as `Leaf Leaf`? Or do we suppress one?
   - Calculate the "repetition rate" of the translation vs. the original.

Output:
- results/audit_v7_4.md: A markdown report with the stats, graphs (ASCII or description), and a PASS/FAIL conclusion for each metric.
