TRACK 119: Grammar Deep Dive - 'sh-' and 't-' prefixes
========================================================

GOAL: Determine the function of the `sh-` and `t-` prefixes.

INPUT:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt`
- `results/master_dictionary_v7_1.json`

TASK 1: Distribution Analysis
-----------------------------
- `sh-`:
  - Hebrew `sh-` = "That/Which" (Relative pronoun).
  - Does it precede verbs or nouns?
  - Compare `shdain` vs `dain`.
- `t-`:
  - Hebrew `t-` = Future tense marker (2nd/3rd person) or "To" (if phonetic).
  - Compare `tchol` vs `chol`.

TASK 2: Contextual Tests
------------------------
- Check occurrences in Recipes vs Narrative.
- If `sh-` is "That", does `shdain` mean "That which is taken"?

OUTPUT FILES:
- `grammar_sh_t_analysis.py`
- `results/grammar_sh_t_report.md`
