Task: Grammar of Prefixes (Prefix Logic)

We suspect the prefixes carry grammatical information (Tense? Case? Marker?).
Now that we can separate them (Task 153), let's analyze their behavior.

Input:
- results/parsed_text.json (From Task 153)

Subtasks:
1. **Positional Analysis:**
   - Do `qo-` words appear at the start of sentences?
   - Do `y-` words appear between nouns?
   - Do `d-` words appear near numbers?

2. **Co-occurrence Matrix:**
   - Which prefixes appear with which roots?
   - Are some roots "Prefix-phobic" (never take a prefix)?
   - Are some roots "Suffix-obligatory"?

3. **Hypothesis Generation:**
   - Propose grammatical functions for the Top 5 prefixes:
     - `qo-`
     - `y-`
     - `o-`
     - `d-`
     - `s-`

Output:
- `results/prefix_grammar_report.md`: The grammar rules we have deduced.
