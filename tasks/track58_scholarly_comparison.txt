TRACK 58: Scholarly Comparison
==============================

GOAL: Compare our findings against PUBLISHED Voynich research.
Are we rediscovering known facts or contradicting experts?

TASK 1: Web Research - Key Theories
-----------------------------------
Search for and document major published theories:
- Stephen Bax's partial decipherment (claimed ~10 words)
- Gerard Cheshire's "proto-Romance" theory
- Nicholas Gibbs's "medieval women's health" theory
- Rene Zandbergen's statistical analyses
- Gordon Rugg's "hoax" hypothesis

TASK 2: Compare Our Findings
----------------------------
For each major scholarly claim:
- Does our analysis SUPPORT or CONTRADICT it?
- Example: Bax claimed "kantaiio" = Centaurea
  - Do we have this word?
  - What do we decode it as?
  - Match or conflict?

TASK 3: Statistical Consensus
-----------------------------
Compare our stats to published analyses:
- Word length distribution
- Character frequency
- Entropy values
- Section differences
Do our numbers match scholarly consensus?

TASK 4: Known Word Comparison
-----------------------------
Several words are "tentatively identified" by scholars:
- Month names in zodiac pages
- Star names in astronomical pages
- Plant names from illustrations

Compare our decodings to these published identifications.

TASK 5: Novel Claims Assessment
-------------------------------
What claims do WE make that are NEW?
- Judeo-Italian hypothesis
- "Cohen" pattern
- Hebrew root structure
Have any scholars proposed similar ideas?

TASK 6: Red Flags Check
-----------------------
Does our research show signs of:
- Overfitting (matching anything to anything)
- Confirmation bias (finding what we expect)
- Circular reasoning (proving assumptions with assumptions)
Compare to critiques of OTHER failed decipherments.
