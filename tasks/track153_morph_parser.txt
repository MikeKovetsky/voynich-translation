Task: Morphological Parser (The Engine)

We need to stop treating 'qokeedy' as a unique word. It is 'qo-keed-y'.
This task will process the entire manuscript and output a "Parsed" version.

Input:
- data/eva_ivtff.txt
- results/morphology_rules.json (The rules we just found)

Subtasks:
1. **Recursive Stripping:**
   - Write `research/morph_parser.py`.
   - For every word in the text:
     - Peel off prefixes from the `morphology_rules.json` list (longest match first).
     - Peel off suffixes.
     - Keep the "Core".
   - Store the result as a structured object: `{"original": "qokeedy", "prefix": ["qo"], "root": "keed", "suffix": ["y"]}`.

2. **Frequency Analysis of Components:**
   - Count the frequency of each Root.
   - Count the frequency of each Prefix/Suffix.
   - **Goal:** Find the "True Top 100 Roots" of the Voynich manuscript.

3. **Output:**
   - `results/parsed_text.json`: The full text in parsed format.
   - `results/root_frequency.csv`: The rank-ordered list of roots.

This is the foundation for the "Grammar" phase.
