Track 229: Recipe Structural Parser

Goal: Analyze the sentence structure of Quire 20 using the new Dictionary v9.1 definitions to identify "Slots" for unknown words.

Input:
- `results/dictionary/dictionary.json` (v9.1).
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Quire 20: f103r-f116v).

Steps:
1. Tokenize the Recipe Section sentences (splitting by `daiin` or newlines).
2. Map every word to its Dictionary Class:
   - `daiin` -> `COMMAND_TAKE`
   - `y` -> `CONNECTOR_AND`
   - `ol` -> `ARTICLE_THE`
   - `qokeedy` -> `NOUN_MIXTURE` (New!)
   - `[Unknown]` -> `UNKNOWN`
3. Generate "Abstract Patterns":
   - Example: `COMMAND_TAKE + ARTICLE_THE + UNKNOWN + CONNECTOR_AND + UNKNOWN`
4. Identify the most common "Slots" where `UNKNOWN` appears.
   - If `UNKNOWN` is always between `ol` and `y`, it is 99% an Ingredient Noun.
   - If `UNKNOWN` is always at the end of a sentence, it is likely a Verb/Process.
5. Output: `results/recipe_structure_v3.json` (The patterns) and `results/structural_guesses.json` (The inferred types of unknowns).
6. Summary: `results/track-229-results_summary.md`.
