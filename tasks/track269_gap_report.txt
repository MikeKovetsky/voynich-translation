Task: The Gap Report (The Hit List)
Goal: Quantify the "Low Coverage" and define the targets for the next phase.
Inputs:
- results/quire20_translation_final.md
- data/transcription.txt

Steps:
1. **Coverage Analysis:**
   - Calculate the % of tokens in Quire 20 that are still generic placeholders (`[PLANT NAME]`, `[UNKNOWN]`, `plant_candidate`).
   - Calculate the % of tokens that are fully translated.
2. **The Hit List:**
   - Identify the Top 500 most frequent *Unknown/Generic* words.
   - Sort by frequency.
3. **Context Extraction:**
   - For the Top 20 unknowns, provide 3 example sentences.
   - e.g., "Word `X` appears in 'Take X', 'Mix X', 'X the water'".
4. **Output:**
   - `results/coverage_gap_report.md`: The statistical reality check.
   - `results/top_500_unknowns.csv`: The backlog for the next agents.
