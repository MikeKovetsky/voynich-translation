Task: Astronomical Label Validation (Track 308)
Goal: Validate the 72 Astro label candidates against Hebrew/Latin month and star names.
Inputs:
- results/track-306-results_summary.md
- results/astronomical_candidates.json

Steps:
1.  **Phonetic Mapping:**
    - Map Voynich characters to phonetic equivalents (`o` -> `a/o`, `k` -> `k/q`, `8` -> `d/t`, etc.).
    - Convert the 72 labels into "Phonetic Strings".

2.  **Fuzzy Match:**
    - Compare phonetic strings to:
        - Hebrew Months: *Nisan, Iyar, Sivan...*
        - Latin Months: *Januarius, Februarius...*
        - Arabic Star Names: *Aldebaran, Altair, Rigel...*

3.  **Outputs:**
    - `results/astro_matches.md`: List of "Likely Matches" with confidence scores.
    - `results/track-308-results_summary.md`: Did we crack the Zodiac names?
