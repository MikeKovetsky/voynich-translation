# Track 51: Expand Dictionary - Semantic Domains

## OBJECTIVE
Systematically expand vocabulary in specific semantic domains:
- Botanical (plants, parts, processes)
- Medical (body parts, conditions, treatments)
- Astronomical (celestial bodies, time)
- Pharmaceutical (ingredients, preparations)

## INPUT FILES
- `results/hybrid_dictionary.json` - Current dictionary
- `results/proto_romance_analysis.json` - Italian/Hebrew matches
- `results/hebrew_analysis.json` - Hebrew patterns
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` - Source text

Current dictionary has:
