Task: Visual-Text Alignment (The "Rosetta" Hunt)
Goal: Identify plants visually using external Medieval Herbals, then find their names in the text.
Inputs:
- images/ (The specific pages)
- results/quire20_translation_final.md (To check ingredient usage)

Steps:
1. **Select Target Pages:** Choose 5-10 pages with very distinctive plants (e.g., f33v, f100r).
2. **External Identification:**
   - Search web/databases for "Voynich f[X] plant identification".
   - Look for consensus (e.g., "f39v is Cirsium (Thistle)").
3. **Internal Search:**
   - Get the Latin, German, and Italian names for that plant (e.g., *Carduus*, *Distel*).
   - Scan the Voynich text on that page.
   - Do any words resemble these names? (e.g., `kard`, `dist`?)
   - Do they resemble specific *properties*? (e.g., "Prickly", "Sharp").
4. **Output:**
   - `results/visual_grounding_candidates.json`: Matches between Page -> Visual ID -> Text Candidate.
   - *Hypothesis:* `choly` might not just be "Nettle-type"; it might be the actual word for Nettle in a specific dialect.
