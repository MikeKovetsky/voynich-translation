TRACK 131: Star Name Decoding (The 'Source' List)
=================================================

GOAL: Determine if "Star Names" in the Zodiac/Cosmo sections are actually lists of ingredients or qualities.

INPUT:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Zodiac star lists)
- `results/master_dictionary_v7_3.json`

TASK 1: Prefix Analysis
-----------------------
- Analyze the "Star names" (labels near stars).
- Do they start with `y-` ("And") or `o-` ("The")?
- If they follow the pattern `o-[Word]` or `y-[Word]`, they are likely lists.

TASK 2: Root Matching
---------------------
- Strip the prefixes. Do the roots match our known ingredients?
- Example: Is there a star named `ochol` (The Leaf) or `ychedy` (And Mixture)?

TASK 3: Hypothesis Test
-----------------------
- Hypothesis: The "Stars" represent specific herbs or essences governing the water.

OUTPUT FILES:
- `star_name_analysis.py`
- `results/star_name_report.md`
