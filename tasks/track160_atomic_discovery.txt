Task: Atomic Root Discovery (De-souping)

The Segmentation (Task 157) helped, but we still have roots like `keeoollkeeeyolainarokeomqokain...`.
This isn't a word. It's a sequence of `kee`, `ol`, `ain`, `ar`.

Input:
- results/segmented_text.txt

Subtasks:
1. **N-gram Analysis of Long Roots:**
   - Filter for "words" longer than 8 characters.
   - Run 2-gram and 3-gram frequency analysis on the *characters* of these words.
   - Identify the building blocks: `ol`, `or`, `dy`, `kee`, `dai`, `chol`.

2. **Recursive Atomizer:**
   - Build a list of "Atoms" (length 2-4).
   - Write a script to greedily split the long strings into Atoms.
   - `keeoollkeeey` -> `kee-ol-l-kee-ey`.

3. **Output:**
   - `results/atomic_text.txt`: The text fully broken down into atoms.
   - `results/atoms_list.json`: The list of valid atoms and their frequencies.
