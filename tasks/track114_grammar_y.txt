TRACK 114: Grammar Refinement - The 'y-' Prefix
===============================================

GOAL: Determine if `y-` is a Plural Marker, a Conjunction ("And"), or something else.

INPUT:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt`
- `results/master_dictionary_v7.json`

TASK 1: List Analysis
---------------------
- Find lists of words (e.g., in recipes or labels).
- Does `y-` appear on the 2nd, 3rd, 4th items? (Indicating "And").
- Example: `chol ychol ychar` ("Leaf and leaf and root"?).

TASK 2: Plural Analysis
-----------------------
- Does `y-` appear when multiple items are drawn?
- Compare `chol` (Leaf) vs `ychol` (Leaves) vs images.

TASK 3: Update Rules
--------------------
- If "And": Update Dictionary with `y-` = Conjunction.
- If Plural: Update Dictionary with `y-` = Plural.

OUTPUT FILES:
- `grammar_y_analysis.py`
- `results/grammar_y_report.md`
