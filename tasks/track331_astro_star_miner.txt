## Goal
The Astro section is at 44% coverage because we are missing Star Names. We will not fuzzy match randomly. We will look for **Structure**.

1.  **Input:** `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Astro Section only: f67-f73).
2.  **Script:** `research/astro_star_miner.py`

    -   **Output:** `results/astro_structure_analysis.md`.

## Expected Output
1.  `results/astro_structure_analysis.md`:
    -   List of "Zodiac Candidates" (appear in 12s).
    -   List of "Planet Candidates" (appear in 7s).
    -   This boosts "Structural Coverage" even if we don't know the specific star name.

## Why?
Tagging `[STAR_NAME]` is better than `[UNKNOWN]`. It makes the text structurally readable.
