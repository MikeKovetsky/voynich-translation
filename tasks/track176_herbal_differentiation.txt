# Task 176: Herbal Noun Differentiation

## Goal
Distinguish between "Plant Names" (Specific) and "Plant Parts" (Generic) within the Herbal Section.

## Input
- `results/noun_clusters.json` (Specifically the 98 "Herbal" clusters from Task 171)
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Herbal pages only)

## Process
1.  **Frequency Distribution:**
    - For each word in the Herbal Clusters:
        - Count distinct pages it appears on.
        - Count total occurrences.
2.  **Classification:**
    - **Label: PLANT_NAME:** High frequency on *one* page (or 1-2 adjacent pages), zero elsewhere. (e.g., "This is the [Rose]").
    - **Label: PLANT_PART:** Medium frequency across *many* pages. (e.g., "Its [leaves] are green").
3.  **Validation:**
    - Check if "Plant Names" appear in the first paragraph (Label position).
    - Check if "Plant Parts" appear in the text body.

## Output
- `results/herbal_taxonomy.json`: Mapping of Word -> Category (Name vs. Part).
- `results/herbal_glossary.md`: List of potential Plant Names vs. Parts.
- `results/track-176-results_summary.md`: Summary.
