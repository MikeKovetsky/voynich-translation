Track 230: The Color Hunt (Visual Correlation)

Goal: Identify words that correlate with specific ink colors (Red/Green/Blue/Yellow) to find color terms or property descriptors (Hot/Cold).

Input:
- `results/color_validation_report.md` (Reference).
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt`.
- *External Knowledge/Assumption:* 
    - Red = Mars/Hot (Folios f25v, f39v, f57v).
    - Blue = Venus/Cold (Folios f13r, f101v, f34v).
    - Green = Nature/Wet (General).

Steps:
1. Define "Color Sets":
   - List folios heavily dominated by Red ink.
   - List folios heavily dominated by Blue ink.
2. Frequency Analysis:
   - Calculate the frequency of *every word* on "Red Pages".
   - Calculate the frequency of *every word* on "Blue Pages".
   - Calculate the global frequency.
3. Identify "Color Spikes":
   - Find words that are >3x more frequent on Red pages than Blue pages (Candidates for "Red", "Fire", "Hot", "Mars").
   - Find words that are >3x more frequent on Blue pages (Candidates for "Blue", "Water", "Cold", "Venus").
4. Cross-reference with Dictionary:
   - Do we already know them? (e.g., `sho` = Fire? `os` = Blue?).
5. Output: `results/color_correlation_candidates.json`.
6. Summary: `results/track-230-results_summary.md`.
