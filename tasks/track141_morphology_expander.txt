Task: Morphological Dictionary Expansion

Goal: Increase dictionary coverage by algorithmically stripping prefixes and suffixes to identify "Roots".

Theory: If `daiin` = `d-` (Take) + `aiin` (Water), then `d-` might be a prefix "Take" or "Imperative".

Steps:
1. Load the full corpus.
2. Identify common prefixes (start of word) and suffixes (end of word) based on frequency.
    - Candidates to test: `d-`, `q-`, `y-`, `s-`, `-dy`, `-ol`.
3. "Stem" the entire vocabulary: Remove these affixes to find the "Root".
4. Group words by Root.
    - Example: `aiin`, `daiin`, `saiin`, `aiiny`.
5. Generate a "Predicted Meaning" report:
    - If `aiin` = Water and `d-` = Take, predict `daiin` = "Take Water".
    - Apply this logic to UNKNOWN roots. If `d-` appears with Unknown Root X (`d-chcthy`), predict "Take X".
6. Output `results/morphology_expansion.md` with a list of potential new vocabulary derived from these combinatorics.

Script Name: `expand_morphology.py`
Output: `results/morphology_expansion.md`
