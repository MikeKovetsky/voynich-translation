Task: High-Frequency Root Solver

We have 5 roots that account for 30% of the text: `dy`, `in`, `ol`, `ey`, `ar`.
We cannot translate the manuscript without knowing what these mean.

Input:
- results/parsed_text.json
- data/images/ (conceptual)

Subtasks:
1. **Context Analysis:**
   - For each target root (`dy`, `in`, `ol`, `ey`, `ar`, `or`, `al`, `ee`):
     - Find top 5 words *preceding* it.
     - Find top 5 words *following* it.
     - Does `dy` appear at the end of sentences? (Suffix-like?).
     - Does `ol` appear before Nouns? (Article?).

2. **Topic Correlation:**
   - Count frequency of these roots on:
     - **Recipe Pages** (f103-f116).
     - **Star Pages** (f67-f73).
     - **Herbal Pages**.
   - Calculate "Topic Bias" (e.g., `air` is 3x more common in Recipes).

3. **Hypothesis Generation:**
   - Assign a provisional meaning to each.
   - Candidates: "The", "And", "It", "Is", "Water", "Star", "Plant", "Mix".

4. **Output:**
   - `results/high_freq_roots.md`: Analysis and proposed meanings.
   - `results/root_proposals.json`: Machine-readable proposals.
