## JUDEO-ITALIAN CHARACTERISTICS

### Linguistic Features
1. Italian base language
2. Hebrew loanwords (especially religious/medical)
3. Hebrew grammatical influences
4. Unique vocabulary for Jewish concepts
5. Sometimes written in Hebrew script

### Examples of Judeo-Italian
- "sciabbàt" (Shabbat) - Sabbath
- "berakhà" (berakha) - blessing
- "mazàl" (mazal) - luck
- "minian" (minyan) - prayer quorum
- "kasherùt" - kosher laws
