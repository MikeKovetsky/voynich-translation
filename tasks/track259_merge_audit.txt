Task: Merge Context Audit
Goal: Prevent "destructive merging" of distinct words by checking their semantic context.
Inputs:
- results/dictionary/master_dictionary_v13.json
- research/consolidate_dictionary.py (The script logic)

Steps:
1. Identify "Fuzzy Candidates" (Levenshtein distance = 1).
2. For each pair (Word A, Word B):
   - **Section Check:** Does Word A appear in the same *Section* (Herb/Astro/Recipe) as Word B?
     - If A is only in Astro and B is only in Recipes -> **REJECT MERGE** (They are likely distinct technical terms).
   - **Grammar Check:** Does the "error" look like a suffix?
     - e.g., `daiin` vs `daiiny`. If `-y` is a known suffix (Imperative), do not merge as a "spelling error". Treat as "Root + Suffix".
3. Filter the merge list.
4. Output `results/safe_merges.json` (The approved list).
5. Generate `track-259-results_summary.md` describing how many merges were rejected and why.
