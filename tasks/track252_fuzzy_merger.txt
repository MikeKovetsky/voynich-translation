Track 252: Fuzzy Merger (Transcription Cleanup)

Goal: Clean up the dictionary by merging "Hapax Legomena" (words appearing once) that are likely typos of high-frequency words.

Input:
- `results/dictionary/dictionary.json` (v12.0).
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt`.

Logic:
- If Word A has frequency 1 and differs by 1 character from Word B (frequency > 50):
- Assume Word A is a typo of Word B.

Steps:
1. Generate frequency list from Corpus.
2. Identify Hapax (Freq=1) words.
3. Find Levenshtein neighbors (dist=1) among High-Freq words.
4. Create Dictionary Aliases:
   - Word A -> Meaning: "Variant of [Word B]"
   - Status: 2 (Translated/Resolved).
5. Output: `results/fuzzy_merges.json`.
