Track 244: Plant Noun Miner

Goal: Mass-identify unknown words that function as Plant names in the Herbal Section.

Input:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Book II: f1-f66).
- `results/dictionary/dictionary.json`.

Logic:
- Scan for patterns: `o-[UNKNOWN]`, `[UNKNOWN] otar` (Green [Plant]), `[UNKNOWN] oteey` (Leafy [Plant]).
- Herbal pages are "Dry" (`o` dominant). Unknowns here are likely plants.

Steps:
1. Extract unknowns from Herbal pages.
2. Prioritize words starting with `o-` or `ch-` (common plant prefixes).
3. Generate a list of `plant_candidate` entries.
4. Output: `results/mining/plant_candidates.json`.
