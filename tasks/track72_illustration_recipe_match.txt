TRACK 72: Recipe-Illustration Cross-Reference
=============================================

GOAL: Match decoded recipes with botanical illustrations.
If we decode "Take fig", is there a fig plant drawn anywhere?

WHY THIS MATTERS:
- We proved illustration matching works (83.3% on botanical)
- Recipe ingredients should match plants in herbal sections
- Cross-validation strengthens confidence

INPUT FILES:
- results/master_dictionary.json
- results/recipe_deep_dive.json (from Track 71)
