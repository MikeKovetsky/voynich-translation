TRACK 100: Herbal Text Grammar Analysis
=======================================

GOAL: Determine if the text blocks on Herbal Pages follow the Recipe Grammar or a new "Descriptive Grammar".

HYPOTHESIS:
- Recipe: Imperative ("Take X")
- Herbal Text: Descriptive ("This is X", "It has Y")

TASK 1: Analyze Paragraph Starts
--------------------------------
Check the first words of text blocks on 20 herbal pages.
- Do they start with `daiin` (Take)?
- Or do they start with nouns/demonstratives?

TASK 2: Grammar Frame Test
--------------------------
Apply the `daiin [OBJ] qok- [SOURCE]` frame to f10r-f30r.
- Does it fit?
- Or do we see a different structure like `[NOUN] [VERB] [ADJECTIVE]`?

TASK 3: Identify Descriptors
----------------------------
Look for adjectives (color, size, taste).
We know `dam`=red (blood). Do we see `dam` on pages with red plants?

OUTPUT FILES:
- herbal_grammar.py
- results/herbal_grammar_report.md
- results/herbal_grammar_structure.json
