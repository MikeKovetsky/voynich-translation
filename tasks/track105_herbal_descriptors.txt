TRACK 105: Herbal Descriptor Mapping
====================================

GOAL: Validate color and size descriptors in the Herbal Section by comparing text to illustrations.

INPUT:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Herbal Pages)
- `images/` (Reference to what we know about images, or use existing mapping files)
- `results/master_dictionary_v6.json`

TASK 1: Color Mapping
---------------------
- Search for `dam` (red) on pages with Red plants/roots.
- Search for `old` (gold/yellow) on pages with Yellow flowers.
- Search for `olk` (white?) or `or` (light?) on pages with White flowers.
- Output a "Hit Rate" for each color word.

TASK 2: Size/Shape Descriptors
------------------------------
- Identify words that correlate with:
  - Big leaves vs Small leaves.
  - Blue roots vs non-blue roots.
- Look for `dal` (big/great) or `par` (small?).

TASK 3: The "This is" Pattern
-----------------------------
- If the text is descriptive, can we find the pattern `[DEMONSTRATIVE] [PLANT_NAME]`?
- Look for words that frequently start the FIRST sentence of a page.

OUTPUT FILES:
- `herbal_descriptors.py`
- `results/herbal_descriptors_report.md`
- `results/descriptor_mapping.json`
