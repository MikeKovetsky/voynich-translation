Task: Contextual Fill-in-the-Blank (Track 303)
Goal: Use the translated "Golden Sentences" to infer the meaning of remaining unknown words.
Inputs:
- results/full_draft_translation_v1.md (or generate v2)
- results/dictionary/master_dictionary_v16.json

Steps:
1.  **Identify "Cloze" Sentences:**
    - Find sentences in the transcription where >75% of words are known.
    - Example: "Take the [UNKNOWN] and mix with Salt."
    - The [UNKNOWN] is likely an ingredient.

2.  **Category Tagging:**
    - If the frame is `ol [UNKNOWN]`, tag as `noun` / `ingredient`.
    - If the frame is `[UNKNOWN]-y`, tag as `verb` / `imperative`.

3.  **Inference:**
    - Generate a list of "Probable Definitions" based on context.

4.  **Outputs:**
    - `results/context_inferred_candidates.json`.
    - `results/track-303-results_summary.md`: List of high-probability guesses.
