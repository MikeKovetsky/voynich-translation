# Task 205: Dosage Detection

## Goal
Identify words that function as "Numbers" or "Dosages" in the recipes.

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Quire 20)

## Process
1.  **Pattern Search:**
    - Look for repeated glyphs (e.g., `iii`, `ii`).
    - Look for words that always appear *before* an ingredient (`[Amount] of [Ingredient]`).
    - Candidates: `dair`? `dal`? (Aries is the 1st sign).
2.  **Frequency Analysis:**
    - Are there words that appear only in the "Dosage Slot" (just before the ingredient)?

## Output
- `results/dosage_candidates.json`: List of potential numbers.
- `results/track-205-results_summary.md`: Summary.
