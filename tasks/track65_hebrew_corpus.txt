TRACK 65: Medieval Hebrew Corpus Expansion
==========================================

GOAL: Expand dictionary using ACTUAL medieval Hebrew medical texts.
Find more vocabulary matches from historical sources.

WHY THIS MATTERS:
- Our Hebrew hypothesis is supported but vocabulary is limited
- Medieval Hebrew medical texts exist and are documented
- Jewish physicians wrote in Hebrew/Judeo-Italian
- More vocabulary = better coverage

INPUT FILES:
- results/clean_dictionary.json
- data/eva_ivtff.txt
- Web research on medieval Hebrew medicine

TARGET SOURCES (search for):
1. Maimonides medical works (12th century)
   - "Treatise on Asthma"
   - "Medical Aphorisms"
   - "Regimen of Health"

2. Sefer ha-Refu'ot (Book of Remedies)
   - Medieval Hebrew medical compilation

3. Sha'ar ha-Shamayim (Gate of Heaven)
   - Hebrew astronomical treatise

4. Hebrew botanical terminology
   - Plant names in Mishnah/Talmud
   - Medieval Hebrew herbals

5. Judeo-Italian medical manuscripts
   - Northern Italian Jewish medical texts
   - 14th-15th century documents

TASK 2: Consonant Skeleton Matching
-----------------------------------
For each Hebrew term:
- Extract consonant skeleton
- Search Voynich corpus for matches
- Validate against context

Example:
- Hebrew: שורש (shoresh = root)
- Skeleton: sh-r-sh
- Voynich matches: shor, shar, shees (already validated!)
