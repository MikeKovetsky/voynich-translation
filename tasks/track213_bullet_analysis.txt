# Task 213: Bullet Analysis (Recipe Starters)

## Goal
Identify the primary "Starter Words" that act as bullet points in the Recipe Section (Quire 20).

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Quire 20)

## Process
1.  **Extract Paragraphs:**
    - Identify every paragraph block in f103r-f116v.
2.  **Analyze First Words:**
    - Extract the 1st word of each paragraph.
    - Count frequency.
3.  **Categorization:**
    - Group them: `saiin`, `daiin`, `ol-`, `qok-`.
    - Do these words ever appear in the *middle* of a sentence? Or only at the start?

## Output
- `results/recipe_starters.csv`: List of starter words and counts.
- `results/track-213-results_summary.md`: Summary.
