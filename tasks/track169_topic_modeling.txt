# Task 169: Topic Modeling (LDA)

## Goal
Discover the "hidden topics" of the manuscript pages using Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA).
This differs from Task 168 by looking at *document-level* (page/paragraph) co-occurrence rather than local window.

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Grouped by page/paragraph)
- `results/translation_v11.md` (For reference of structure)

## Process
1. **Document Prep:** Treat each *Page* or *Paragraph* as a "Document".
2. **Tokenization:** Use the *Root* of the words (strip `o-`, `y-`, `daiin-`, etc.).
3. **LDA Model:** Run LDA with `num_topics` = 5, 10, 20.
4. **Topic Interpretation:** Look at the top words for each topic.
   - Do they correlate with the illustrations (Herbal vs. Biological vs. Cosmological)?
5. **Mapping:** Assign each page a primary topic.

## Output
- `results/topic_model.json`: Topics and their top words. Page -> Topic mapping.
- `results/topic_distribution.md`: A report correlating topics with manuscript sections (Herbal, etc.).
- `results/track-169-results_summary.md`: Summary of findings.
