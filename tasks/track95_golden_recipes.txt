TRACK 95: Golden Recipe Generation
==================================

GOAL: Produce the most readable English translations possible for the top 20 recipes.

INPUT:
- `results/master_dictionary_v5.json` (with measurements)
- Grammar Frame: `daiin [AMOUNT] [INGREDIENT] qok- [SOURCE]`

TASK 1: Select Candidates
-------------------------
Filter recipe lines (f103-f116) for:
1. Presence of `daiin` (or `sain`, `dain`)
2. Presence of known Ingredient (`chol`, `char`, `chl`, `otaiin`, etc.)
3. Presence of `qok-` preposition
4. Presence of Measurement (`ar`, `al`, `aiin`) OR known structure

TASK 2: Synthesize Translations
-------------------------------
For the top 20 lines, produce a polished output.

Raw: `daiin ar chol qokaiin otaiin`
Literal: Take handful leaf of fig
Polished: "Take a handful of fig leaves."

TASK 3: Coherence Scoring
-------------------------
Rate each translation:
- High: All 4 slots filled with meaningful words.
- Med: 3 slots filled.
- Low: <3 slots filled.

OUTPUT FILES:
- generate_golden_recipes.py
- results/golden_recipes.md (The Showcase File)
- results/golden_recipes.json (Structured data)
