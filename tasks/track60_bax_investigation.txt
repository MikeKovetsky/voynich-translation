TRACK 60: Bax Word Investigation
=================================

GOAL: Investigate why our readings differ from Stephen Bax's claimed decodings.
Who is right? Are we reading different things?

BACKGROUND:
Stephen Bax (2014) claimed to decode ~10 words by matching illustrations to names.
Track 58 found our labels DON'T match his readings:
- f17r: Bax says "kantairon" (centaury), we have "fshody"
- f5r: Bax says "kaur" (hellebore), we have "kshody"

INPUT FILES:
- data/eva_ivtff.txt
- images/f17r.jpg, images/f5r.jpg (if available)
- results/hybrid_dictionary.json

TASK 1: Web Research - Bax's Method
-----------------------------------
Search for Stephen Bax's original paper/blog:
- What EXACTLY did he claim?
- Which EVA characters did he identify?
- What was his phonetic mapping?

Document his claimed character values:
| EVA char | Bax value | Our value | Match? |
|----------|-----------|-----------|--------|

TASK 2: Extract Our Labels
--------------------------
For each Bax folio (f2v, f5r, f17r, f25v, f33v, f66r, f68r3):
- Extract the FIRST word on the page (often plant label)
- Extract label near the plant illustration
- Document in EVA transcription

TASK 3: Character-by-Character Comparison
-----------------------------------------
Compare Bax's readings to ours:

Example for f17r:
- Bax: "kantairon" → k-a-n-t-a-i-r-o-n
- Our EVA: "fshody" → f-sh-o-d-y
- Are these even the SAME word? Same position?

TASK 4: Transcription System Check
----------------------------------
Bax may have used different transcription:
- Did he use EVA? Currier? Something else?
- Our system: EVA from eva_ivtff.txt
- Could differences be transcription artifacts?

TASK 5: Visual Verification
---------------------------
For each disputed word:
- Look at the manuscript image
- What characters are ACTUALLY there?
- Is Bax reading a different word/position?

TASK 6: Assessment
------------------
Possible conclusions:
1. We're reading DIFFERENT words (different positions on page)
2. Transcription system difference
3. One of us misread the characters
4. Both could be partially correct
