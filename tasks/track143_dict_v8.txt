TRACK 143: Dictionary Update v8.0 (Clustered Vocabulary)
========================================================

GOAL: Integrate the findings from the Optimization Phase into a definitive Dictionary v8.0.

INPUT:
- `results/master_dictionary_v7_4.json`
- `results/word_clusters.json` (Clustering results)
- `results/synonym_candidates.json` (Synonyms)
- `results/semantic_field_candidates.json` (Colors)

TASK 1: Integration Strategy
----------------------------
- **High Confidence Clusters:** Update words with >0.7 confidence. Use the category as the meaning if exact meaning is unknown (e.g., "Meaning: [Plant Name]").
- **Synonyms:** Merge synonym entries. Point `qol` to `ol`.
- **Colors:** Add "Color (Blue?)" to the top blue candidates.

TASK 2: Dictionary Cleaning
---------------------------
- Consolidate duplicate entries.
- Ensure grammar markers (`sh-`, `y-`) are consistent.

OUTPUT FILES:
- `update_dict_v8.py`
- `results/master_dictionary_v8_0.json`
- `results/dictionary_v8_report.md`
