TRACK 84: Build Master Dictionary v3.0 (The "Verified" Edition)
=============================================================

GOAL: Consolidate all massive findings from Phase 24 into a new Master Dictionary.

INPUTS:
- results/master_dictionary.json (Current base)
- results/full_herbal_mining.json (Track 80 - 407 entries)
- results/plant_pair_validation.json (Track 83 - 54 ULTRA-HIGH confidence entries)

LOGIC:
1. Priority Hierarchy (Highest to Lowest):
   - Level 1: ULTRA_HIGH confidence (Track 83 pairs) -> Overwrites ALL.
   - Level 2: HIGH confidence (Track 80 unique mining) -> Overwrites generics.
   - Level 3: EXISTING dictionary entries -> Kept if no conflict.

2. Processing:
   - Load all sources.
   - For each word, assign a "confidence score" (0-100).
   - Resolve conflicts automatically based on score.
   - Tag source for every entry (e.g., "Track83_PlantPair").

3. Validation:
   - Calculate new coverage stats for Herbal vs Recipe sections.
   - We expect herbal coverage to jump significantly.

OUTPUTS:
- update_dict_v3.py
- results/master_dictionary_v3.json (The new gold standard)
- results/dictionary_v3_report.md:
  - Total entries
  - Distribution by confidence
  - Coverage improvement analysis
