TRACK 93: Identify Recipe Measurements
======================================

GOAL: Identify words that function as measurements (amounts, durations) based on their grammatical position.

HYPOTHESIS:
Measurements likely appear:
1. Before the ingredient: "Take [Amount] [Ingredient]"
2. After the ingredient: "Take [Ingredient] [Amount]"
3. At the end of instruction: "...boil for [Time]"

TASK 1: Analyze "daiin" Collocations
------------------------------------
Look at words appearing between `daiin` and the confirmed ingredient (`char`/`chol`/`chl`).
Pattern: `daiin [X] char` -> Is [X] a number or amount?

TASK 2: Analyze Repetitive Endings
----------------------------------
Look for words that end many recipe lines.
Candidates for "mix well", "drink", "apply".

TASK 3: Clustering
------------------
Cluster words that appear in the same syntactic slots.
- Slot A: Ingredient (char, chol, chl)
- Slot B: Connector (qok-, ar)
- Slot C: Measurement?
