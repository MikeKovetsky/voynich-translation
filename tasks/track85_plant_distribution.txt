# Track 85: Strict Plant Validation (Distribution Check)

## Problem
We identified 54 "Ultra-High Confidence" plant names because they appear on plant pages AND recipes.
However, if `ckhal` (Ricinus) also appears in the **Zodiac** or **Cosmological** sections, our hypothesis might be wrong (unless it's a star name too).

## Hypothesis
True plant names should be **concentrated** in:
1. Botanical Section
2. Recipe/Pharma Section
They should be **absent** or **rare** in:
1. Zodiac Section
2. Cosmological Section

## Tasks
1. **Load the 54 Ultra-High words** from `results/plant_pair_validation.json`.
2. **Check Distribution**: Calculate frequency of each word per section (Herbal, Astro, Bio, Recipe, Cosmo).
3. **Calculate Specificity Score**: 
   - `Score = (Freq_Herbal + Freq_Recipe) / Total_Freq`
   - High score (>0.8) = Likely a real plant/ingredient.
   - Low score (<0.5) = Likely a common word ("red", "leaf", "root", "small").
4. **Flag "Leaking" Words**: Identify "plant names" that appear frequently in Zodiac/Cosmo sections.

## Expected Output
- `results/plant_distribution_check.json`: Distribution data for all 54 words.
- `results/plant_specificity_report.md`: Validated list of *truly* specific plant terms.
