TRACK 138: Validation - Cross-Check Plant Locations with Recipes
===============================================================

GOAL: Validate the internal consistency of the manuscript.

INPUT:
- `results/ingredient_map.json` (Where plants are drawn)
- `results/recipe_ingredients_v2.json` (Where plants are used)

TASK 1: Proximity Check
-----------------------

- Find all recipes using `chtol`.
- Calculate the "distance" (in folios) between the drawing and the recipe.
- Hypothesis: Recipes are grouped near the relevant plants (or in a separate "Pharmacy" section).

TASK 2: Section Analysis
------------------------

- Or are they used in the *Bio* section?

OUTPUT FILES:
