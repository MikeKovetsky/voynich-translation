# Track 316: Sentence Starter Hunt (Particles & Pronouns)
# Status: Ready for Execution
# Priority: High

## Goal
Identify the "Sentence Starters". Our previous analysis showed "Verbs" appear in Position 3. This implies Position 1 and 2 are occupied by something else—likely Particles, Pronouns, or Conjunctions. We need to identify them to correct our Sentence Parser.

## Requirements
1.  **Input:** `data/eva_ivtff.txt`.
2.  **Script:** `research/starter_hunt.py`
    -   Scan all lines in the corpus.
    -   Identify words that appear at `Position 1` (Start of line) with high frequency.
    -   Calculate the **Start Probability**: `Count(Start) / Count(Total)`.
    -   Filter for words with `Total Count > 50` and `Start Probability > 40%`.

## Expected Output
1.  `results/sentence_starters.md`:
    -   List of Top 20 "Starter Words".
    -   Hypothesis for each: "The", "And", "If", "Then", "He/She".
    -   Do these align with the "Star/Eye" words (`aiin`, `saiin`, `sho`)?

## Why?
If we know what starts the sentence, we can strip it away and find the *real* Subject and Verb.
