# Task 211: Quantitative Translation

## Goal
Test the validity of the new "Measurement Dictionary" by translating 20 recipes to see if they produce logical instructions (e.g., "Take 2 Cups").

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Quire 20)
- `results/dictionary/dictionary_v9_4.json`

## Process
1.  **Select Recipes:** Pick 20 paragraphs from f103r-f116v that contain `saiin`, `ar`, or `ii`.
2.  **Translate:**
    - Apply the full dictionary.
    - Look for structure: `[Verb] [Amount] [Unit] [Ol] [Ingredient]`.
3.  **Evaluation:**
    - Does it read like: "Take 2 Handfuls of [Plant]"?
    - Or does it fail?

## Output
- `results/quantitative_recipes.md`: 20 Translated recipes.
- `results/track-211-results_summary.md`: Summary.
