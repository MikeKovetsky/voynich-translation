TRACK 123: 'Chedy' Investigation - Drink vs Plant
===================================================

GOAL: Determine if `chedy` means "Plant/Herb", "Drink", or both.

INPUT:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt`
- `results/master_dictionary_v7_1.json`

TASK 1: Distribution Analysis
-----------------------------
- **Recipe End:** Frequency of `chedy` as the *last* word vs *first* word.
- **Herbal Labels:** Does `chedy` appear as a label for a plant? (Strong evidence for "Plant").
- **Phrasing:** `daiin chedy` (Take plant) vs `qokeey chedy` (Cook plant) vs `chedy` (Drink!).

TASK 2: Morphological Check
---------------------------
- `qokchedy` (With the ...): Fits "Plant" ("With the plant") and "Drink" ("With the drink").
- `lchedy` (To the ...): "To the plant" or "To the drink".
- `chedy` vs `sheedy` (Honey?): Check for confusion.

OUTPUT FILES:
- `chedy_analysis.py`
- `results/chedy_investigation_report.md`
