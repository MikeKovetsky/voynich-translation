# Task 207: Number Pattern Search

## Goal
Identify the full set of numbers (1-10) by looking for sequential patterns in the text labels.

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Astro & Cosmo sections)
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Raw transcription for glyph analysis)

## Process
1.  **Star Ring Analysis:**
    - Find pages with concentric rings of stars (e.g., f68r).
    - Extract the labels of these stars.
    - Are they sequential? (e.g., `o`, `y`, `d`...?).
2.  **Glyph Sequence:**
    - Look for `i`, `ii`, `iii`, `iiii` (or `iv`).
    - Look for `d`, `r`, `s` sequences.
3.  **Zodiac Degree Marks:**
    - Do the Zodiac pages have 30 labels (for 30 degrees)?

## Output
- `results/number_system.json`: Proposed mapping of glyphs to numbers.
- `results/track-207-results_summary.md`: Summary.
