Task: Syntax Mapper (Function Words)
Goal: Define the grammatical roles of high-frequency "glue" words.
Inputs:
- data/transcription.txt
- results/dictionary/master_dictionary_v14.json (or v13)

Steps:
1. Identify the "Function Words":
   - Criteria: Frequency > 50, Length <= 3 chars (e.g., `y`, `o`, `s`, `d`, `qol`, `or`, `daiin`).
2. Analyze their **Positional Probability**:
   - P(Start of Sentence)
   - P(End of Sentence)
   - P(Precedes Noun)
   - P(Follows Verb)
3. Classify them:
   - **Conjunction:** Appears between two clauses (high P(Start) or P(Mid)).
   - **Preposition:** Precedes Nouns (high P(Precedes Noun)).
   - **Particle/Suffix:** Appears at end of words (if analyzing tokens) or lines.
   - **Pronoun:** Precedes Verbs.
4. Output `results/syntax_map_v1.json`:
   - Format: `{"word": "y", "role": "Conjunction (And)", "confidence": 0.8, "evidence": "..."}`
5. Generate `track-258-results_summary.md`.
