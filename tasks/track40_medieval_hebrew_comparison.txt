# Track 40: Medieval Hebrew Text Comparison

## OBJECTIVE
Compare Voynich text with actual medieval Hebrew manuscripts, particularly
medical and botanical texts, to test the Hebrew hypothesis.

## BACKGROUND
Track 37 showed Hebrew patterns score highest (0.415).
Medieval Hebrew medical/botanical texts existed and could be a source.

## HISTORICAL CONTEXT

### Medieval Hebrew Medical Texts
- **Sefer ha-Refu'ot** - Book of Remedies
- **Asaph ha-Rofe** - 6th-7th century medical encyclopedia
- **Works translated from Arabic** - Many medical texts

### Medieval Hebrew in Italy
- Large Jewish communities in Northern Italy
- Hebrew-Italian bilingualism common
- Judeo-Italian (Hebrew script for Italian)
- Medical practitioners often Jewish

### Voynich Provenance
- Carbon-dated to early 15th century
- Possibly from Northern Italy
- Fits timeline and location for Hebrew influence

### 6. Kabbalistic Pattern Search

Test if Voynich shows these patterns.

### 7. Judeo-Romance Hypothesis
