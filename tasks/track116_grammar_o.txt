TRACK 116: Grammar Analysis - The 'o-' Prefix
===============================================

GOAL: Determine the function of the `o-` / `ol-` prefix.

INPUT:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt`
- `results/master_dictionary_v7_1.json`

TASK 1: Distribution Analysis
-----------------------------
- Where does `o-` appear?
- Labels? (High frequency might imply "The").
- Beginning of sentences?
- Before specific noun types?

TASK 2: Plurality Test
----------------------
- Check labels of plural objects (stars, leaves).
- Do they start with `o-`? (e.g. `ochol` on a page with many leaves?).

TASK 3: Definite Article Test
-----------------------------
- Check for `daiin o-[Ingredient]` vs `daiin [Ingredient]`.
- "Take *the* root" vs "Take root".

OUTPUT FILES:
- `grammar_o_analysis.py`
- `results/grammar_o_report.md`
