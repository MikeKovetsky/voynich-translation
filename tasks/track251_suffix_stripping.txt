Track 251: Suffix Stripping v2

Goal: Mass-translate unknown complex words by stripping common grammatical suffixes and mapping to known roots.

Input:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt`.
- `results/dictionary/dictionary.json` (v12.0).

Focus Suffixes:
- `-dy`: Very common (Adjective? Noun marker?).
- `-ol`: Chemical/Derivative marker?
- `-y`: Imperative/Action?
- `-m`: Plural?

Steps:
1. Identify all "Unknown" words in the text.
2. Iterate through suffixes. Strip them.
3. Check if `stem` is in Dictionary.
4. If yes, create new entry:
   - Word: `[stem] + [suffix]`
   - Meaning: `[Meaning of Stem] (modified)`
   - Status: 1 (Proposed).
5. Output: `results/suffix_mining.json`.
