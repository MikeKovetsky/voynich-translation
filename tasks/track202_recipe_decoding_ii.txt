# Task 202: Recipe Decoding II

## Goal
Re-translate specific recipes using our new semantic knowledge (`choly`=Mars Plant, `ordaiin`=Golden Extract).

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Recipes)
- `results/dictionary/dictionary.json` (v9.2)

## Process
1.  **Select Recipes:** Find recipes containing `choly` or `ald`.
2.  **Translate:**
    - Apply "Imperative" grammar.
    - Substitute known nouns.
3.  **Logic Check:**
    - "Take [Mars Plant]..." -> Does the rest of the recipe describe "Heating" or "Cutting"?
    - "Take [Golden Extract]..." -> Does it describe "Mixing"?

## Output
- `results/recipe_translations_v4.md`: New translations.
- `results/track-202-results_summary.md`: Summary.
