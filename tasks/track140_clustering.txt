TRACK 140: Low-Frequency Word Clustering
========================================

GOAL: Identify the meaning of rare words by clustering them based on their context.

INPUT:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Full Corpus)
- `results/master_dictionary_v7_4.json`

TASK 1: Context Vectorization
-----------------------------
- For every word in the text, create a "context signature" (the 2 words before and 2 words after).
- Focus on the **Unknown** words.

TASK 2: Cluster Analysis
------------------------
- Group unknown words that share similar contexts with **Known** words.
- Example: If `chol` (Leaf) appears after `daiin` and before `qokeey`, and `unknown_word` also appears there, `unknown_word` is likely an **Ingredient**.

TASK 3: Category Assignment
---------------------------
- Assign categories to unknown words: "Ingredient", "Process Verb", "Star Name".
- This doesn't give the exact definition, but it gives the **Type**, which improves translation readability.

OUTPUT FILES:
- `cluster_unknowns.py`
- `results/word_clusters.json`
- `results/clustering_report.md`
