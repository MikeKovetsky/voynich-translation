Task: Root Etymology Hunt
Goal: Link Voynich "True Roots" to historical languages.
Inputs:
- results/dictionary/master_dictionary_v14.json
- results/syntax_map_v1.json (To ignore function words)

Steps:
1. **Extract Roots:**
   - Filter Dictionary v14 for words with `confidence < 0.5` (The Unknowns).
   - Strip suffixes defined in Task 258 (`-y`, `-ol`, `-dy`).
   - Create a list of `Clean_Roots`.
2. **Database Comparison:**
   - Compare `Clean_Roots` against:
     - **Latin Abbreviations** (Cappelli's dictionary).
     - **Medieval Herbal Glossary** (Synonyms of common herbs).
     - **Romance Roots** (Proto-Romance reconstruction).
3. **Matching Logic:**
   - Look for *Consonantal Skeletons* (e.g., `kr` = `cor`? `ml` = `mel`?).
   - Look for *Abbreviation Patterns* (e.g., `q` prefix = `con-`/`com-`?).
4. **Output:**
   - `results/etymology_candidates.csv`: Root | Candidate Language | Candidate Meaning | Confidence.
