TRACK 70: High-Frequency Unknown Analysis
=========================================

GOAL: Analyze the most common UNTRANSLATED words to expand dictionary.
These are the words blocking us from higher coverage.

WHY THIS MATTERS:
- We have 50.9% coverage on Language B
- The other 49.1% contains high-frequency words we don't know
- Cracking even 10-20 of these could significantly boost coverage

- results/master_dictionary.json

TASK 2: Pattern Analysis
------------------------
For each unknown word:
- What's the consonant skeleton?
- Does it have qo- prefix? -y suffix?
- What section does it appear in most?
- What words appear next to it?

TASK 3: Context Clustering
--------------------------
Group unknowns by context:
- Words appearing near "heart" = likely medical
- Words appearing near plants = likely botanical
- Words appearing near numbers = likely measurements
- Words appearing near "priest" = likely religious/procedural

TASK 4: Hebrew/Italian Matching
-------------------------------
For each high-freq unknown:
- Search for Hebrew consonant matches
- Search for Italian consonant matches
- Score potential meanings

Example:
| Unknown | Skeleton | Hebrew Match | Italian Match |
|---------|----------|--------------|---------------|

TASK 5: Propose New Entries
---------------------------

TASK 6: Validate Proposals
--------------------------
For each proposed meaning:
- Does it make sense in context?
- Does it fit the section theme?
- Any conflicts with existing entries?
