TRACK 111: Root Analysis of 'chol' and 'char'
=============================================

GOAL: Determine the meaning of the high-frequency roots `chol` and `char`.

INPUT:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt`
- `results/master_dictionary_v7.json`

TASK 1: Morphology Check
------------------------
- Find all variations: `qokchol`, `dchol`, `ychol`, `ochol`.
- Find all variations: `qokchar`, `dchar`, `ychar`, `ochar`.
- Do they take the `qok-` (Bio) prefix? (e.g. "With the leaves").

TASK 2: Visual Correlation (Herbal Pages)
-----------------------------------------
- `chol`:
  - Count occurrences on pages with **Big Leaves** vs **Small Leaves**.
  - Count occurrences on pages with **No Leaves** (Roots only).
- `char`:
  - Count occurrences on pages with **Flowers** vs **No Flowers**.
  - Count occurrences on pages with **Roots** vs **No Roots**.

TASK 3: Hypothesis Generation
-----------------------------
- `chol` = Leaf? (Hebrew *Aleh*? or *Chol* = Sand/Blue?)
- `char` = Root? (Hebrew *Iqar*? or *Char* = Hole?)
- `char` = Flower?

OUTPUT FILES:
- `root_investigation.py`
- `results/root_chol_char_report.md`
- `results/chol_char_stats.json`
