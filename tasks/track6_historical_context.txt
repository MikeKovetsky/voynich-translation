================================================================================
TRACK 6: HISTORICAL-LINGUISTIC CONTEXT RESEARCH
================================================================================

GOAL:
Research languages plausible for a manuscript dated 1404-1438 to narrow down
the linguistic candidates based on historical and geographical constraints.

================================================================================
BACKGROUND CONTEXT:
================================================================================

The Voynich Manuscript facts:
- Carbon-dated to 1404-1438 (vellum production date)
- Art style suggests Northern Italian or Central European origin
- First confirmed owner: Rudolf II of Prague (1576-1612) - purchased for 600 ducats
- Passed through John Dee, Athanasius Kircher
- Contains: herbal, astronomical, biological, cosmological sections

Our analysis suggests:
- Agglutinative language structure
- Case system similar to Turkish/Finnish/Basque
- Botanical/medical content
- Possibly encrypted or unusual writing system

================================================================================
SPECIFIC TASKS:
================================================================================

1. REGIONAL LANGUAGE SURVEY (1400s)
   
   Map languages spoken in potential origin regions:
   
   a) Northern Italy:
      - Latin (scholarly), Venetian, Lombard, Tuscan
      - Hebrew (Jewish communities)
      - Greek (Byzantine influence)
   
   b) Central Europe (Bohemia/Prague):
      - Czech, German, Latin
      - Yiddish (Jewish communities)
      
   c) Iberian Peninsula:
      - Catalan, Aragonese, Castilian
      - Basque (AGGLUTINATIVE - matches our structure!)
      - Arabic (Moorish influence)
      - Ladino (Jewish Spanish)
   
   d) Eastern Europe:
      - Hungarian (AGGLUTINATIVE - matches!)
      - Polish, Romanian
      - Old Church Slavonic
   
   e) Caucasus/Persia:
      - Georgian (AGGLUTINATIVE!)
      - Armenian
      - Persian/Pahlavi (referenced in arxiv paper)

2. EXTINCT/RARE LANGUAGE RESEARCH
   
   Investigate languages that existed in 1400s but are now extinct or rare:
   - Etruscan (extinct but influence remained)
   - Dalmatian Romance
   - Various Jewish languages (Zarphatic, Judeo-Italian)
   - Cathar-related languages
   - Romani dialects of 15th century
   - Old Hungarian (pre-reform)
   - Pre-standardized Basque dialects

3. RUDOLF II COURT ANALYSIS
   
   Research Rudolf II's court (where manuscript surfaced):
   - Court languages: German, Latin, Czech
   - Rudolf's interests: alchemy, occult, natural sciences
   - Scholars at his court: John Dee, Edward Kelley
   - Manuscripts in his collection (for comparison)

4. HERBAL/MEDICAL TEXT COMPARISON
   
   Research 15th-century herbal texts in various languages:
   - Latin herbals (standard reference)
   - Arabic medical texts translated to Latin
   - Vernacular herbals (German, Italian)
   - Compare structure with Voynich layout

5. SCRIPT/WRITING SYSTEM RESEARCH
   
   Investigate unusual scripts from the period:
   - Glagolitic (Slavic liturgical)
   - Hebrew square script and cursive variations
   - Arabic script adaptations
   - Georgian Mkhedruli
   - Armenian alphabet
   - Invented/constructed scripts of the period

6. COMMUNITY/GROUP ANALYSIS
   
   Research groups that might have used secret writing:
   - Alchemist communities
   - Jewish mystical groups (Kabbalah)
   - Heretical sects (Cathars, Bogomils)
   - Medical/pharmaceutical guilds
   - Diplomatic/mercantile codes
