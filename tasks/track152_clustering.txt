Task: Contextual Clustering (Synonym Finder)

We have many words that appear in the same context as "Water" or "Mix" but are currently untranslated.

Input Files:
- data/voynich_transcription.txt

Subtasks:
1. **Word Embeddings:**
   - Train a simple Word2Vec or FastText model on the full Voynich text (EVA transcription).
   - Window size: 3 (local context is tight).
   - Vector size: 50.

2. **Synonym Extraction:**
   - For each of our "Known Keys" (e.g., `aiin` [Water], `okeol` [Boil], `chol` [Leaf]):
     - Find the top 5 closest vectors in the embedding space.
     - Are these neighbors currently in our dictionary?
     - If NO, they are strong candidates for synonyms.
     - Example: If `daiin` is close to `aiin`, maybe `daiin` is also "Water" or "Liquid".

3. **Cluster Report:**
   - Group the vocabulary into "Semantic Clusters" (e.g., The "Liquid" cluster, The "Plant Part" cluster).

Output:
- results/semantic_clusters.json: The clusters.
- results/synonym_candidates.md: A list of unknown words that are effectively synonyms of known words, ready for human review.
