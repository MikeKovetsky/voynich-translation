Task: Grammar Density Map (Track 280)
Goal: Determine which sections of the manuscript follow the "Recipe Grammar" (Imperative + Object).
Inputs:
- data/voynich_transcription.txt
- results/dictionary/master_dictionary_v15.json

Steps:
1. **Define Markers:**
   - Imperative Suffix: `-y` (at end of word).
   - Ingredient Prefix: `ol-` (at start of word).
   - Process Suffix: `-ed` (at end of word).
   - Standard Command: `daiin` (exact match).

2. **Analyze Corpus:**
   - Iterate through every page in the transcription.
   - Calculate the "Recipe Grammar Density" score for each page:
     - Score = (Count of Markers) / (Total Words on Page).

3. **Classify Sections:**
   - Group pages into "High Density" (Recipes), "Medium Density" (Mixed), and "Low Density" (Narrative/Descriptive).
   - Check if the "Herbal Section" (Quires 1-16) has high or low density.

4. **Outputs:**
   - `results/grammar_density_map.json`: { "f103r": 0.45, "f1r": 0.05, ... }
   - `results/track-280-results_summary.md`: Analysis of the map. Which quires are "Instructional"?
