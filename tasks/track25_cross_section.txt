# Track 25: Cross-Section Validation

## OBJECTIVE
Test if the phonetic mapping developed for the botanical section works on other manuscript sections (astronomical, biological/nymphs).

## BACKGROUND
All analysis so far focused on botanical section (f1r-f57r). The Voynich has other sections:
- **Astronomical** (f67r-f73v): Zodiac diagrams, star charts
- **Biological** (f75r-f84v): "Nymphs" in pools/tubes
- **Cosmological** (f85r-f86v): Circular diagrams
- **Pharmaceutical** (f88r-f102v): Containers, recipes
- **Recipes** (f103r-f116v): Text-only pages

If the same cipher was used throughout, the mapping should work on all sections.

## INPUT FILES
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` - Full EVA transcription (all sections)
- `voynich_raw.txt` - Full Claston transcription
- `results/master_dictionary.json` - From Track 24 (if available)
- `results/transcription_mapping.json` - EVA↔Claston conversion
- `results/botanical_decoded.json` - For comparison

## TASKS

### 1. Section Extraction
Extract text from each section:
- Astronomical (f67r-f73v)
- Biological (f75r-f84v)
- Pharmaceutical (f88r-f102v)
- Recipes (f103r-f116v)

### 2. Apply Phonetic Mapping
For each section:
- Decode all words using confirmed phonetic key
- Calculate Latin match rate
- Compare with botanical section (99.2%)

### 3. Section-Specific Analysis

#### Astronomical Section
- Look for star names (Sirius, Spica, etc.)
- Look for month names (Latin months)
- Look for zodiac names (Aries, Taurus, etc.)
- Compare decoded words with astronomical vocabulary

#### Biological Section
- Look for body part terms (manus, caput, etc.)
- Look for water/liquid terms (aqua, humor)
- Look for medical terms

#### Pharmaceutical Section
- Look for preparation terms (recipe, misce, coque)
- Look for ingredient lists
- Compare structure with botanical recipes

#### Recipes Section
- Pure text analysis
- Look for recipe structure
- Compare with botanical text patterns

### 4. Vocabulary Comparison
- Find words unique to each section
- Find words shared across sections
- Calculate vocabulary overlap with botanical

### 5. Statistical Comparison
For each section calculate:
- Total words
- Unique words
- Latin match rate
- Word length distribution
- Character frequency

### 6. Consistency Check
Verify:
- Same phonetic mapping works
- Same abbreviation system applies
- Same grammar structure present
