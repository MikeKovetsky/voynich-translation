Task: Implement Stolfi's "First Word" Hypothesis Analysis

Goal: Identify probable plant names by analyzing the first word of every page and paragraph in the Herbal Section (Quire 13, roughly f75-f84, but check all 'herbal' visual pages).

Steps:
1. Identify all pages in the `herbal` category (images showing full page plants).
2. Extract the **First Word** of the first paragraph on each of these pages.
3. Extract the **First Word** of the page (if distinct from paragraph start).
4. Compare these "Title Words" against the rest of the corpus:
    - Are they unique to their page? (Strong indicator of a Proper Noun/Label).
    - Do they appear in the "Recipes" section? (This would link the Plant to the Cure).
5. Output a report `results/stolfi_analysis.md` listing:
    - Page ID
    - Candidate Plant Name (First Word)
    - Frequency in Corpus
    - Occurrences in Recipe Section (with context).

Script Name: `analyze_stolfi_labels.py`
Output: `results/stolfi_analysis.md`
