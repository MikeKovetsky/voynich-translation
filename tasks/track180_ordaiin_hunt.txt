# Task 180: The "ordaiin" Hunt

## Goal
Identify the botanical or chemical identity of `ordaiin`, the most frequent ingredient in the manuscript.

## Input
- `results/herbal_taxonomy.json`
- `results/parsed_text.json`

## Process
1.  **Locate Definitions:**
    - Find every page in the **Herbal Section** where `ordaiin` appears.
    - *Critical:* Does it appear in the "Label" position (first word/paragraph)?
2.  **Visual Correlation:**
    - If it appears on Page X, what does the plant look like?
    - Is it the "Sunflower"? The "Lotus"?
3.  **Etymology Check:**
    - Break it down: `or` (Gold/Light?) + `daiin` (Preposition "of" or "from"?).
    - Does it mean "Of Gold"? "Golden Plant"?

- `results/ordaiin_profile.md`: Full report on this specific word.
