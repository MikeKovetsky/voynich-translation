================================================================================
TRACK 3: ASTRONOMICAL SECTION ANALYSIS
================================================================================

GOAL:
Analyze the astronomical section of the Voynich Manuscript to identify 
star names, constellation references, and zodiac labels that could serve
as anchor words for decipherment.

================================================================================
BACKGROUND CONTEXT:
================================================================================

The astronomical section (folios 67r-73v and scattered throughout) contains:
- Circular diagrams with radiating text
- Zodiac symbols (identifiable: Aries, Taurus, Pisces, etc.)
- Star-like patterns and celestial imagery
- Possible constellation maps

Star names are excellent decipherment anchors because:
1. Medieval star names were standardized (Arabic/Latin)
2. They're proper nouns that should be phonetically preserved
3. Zodiac positions might match specific names

================================================================================
INPUT DATA AVAILABLE:
================================================================================

1. Voynich transcription pages for astronomical section:
   - Pages 67r-73v in /voynich_raw.txt
   - Extract using grep: <67r through <73v

2. Known zodiac positions in manuscript:
   - f70v-f73v: zodiac pages with nymphs
   - Each section has ~30 text labels around figures

3. Medieval star catalogs for comparison:
   - Ptolemy's Almagest (star names in Greek/Latin/Arabic)
   - Al-Sufi's Book of Fixed Stars
   - Alfonso X's Libro del Saber

================================================================================
SPECIFIC TASKS:
================================================================================

1. EXTRACT ASTRONOMICAL TEXT
   - Parse all text from folios 67r-73v
   - Identify text that appears near circular patterns
   - Separate "label" text (short, isolated) from "descriptive" text

2. ZODIAC LABEL ANALYSIS
   - For each zodiac sign page, extract the ~30 labels around figures
   - Compare word patterns across zodiac sections
   - Look for 12 repeated words that might be month names

3. STAR NAME MATCHING
   Create phonetic mappings and test against star names:
   
   Major stars to test:
   - Aldebaran (α Tau) - in Taurus section?
   - Regulus (α Leo) - in Leo section?
   - Spica (α Vir) - in Virgo section?
   - Antares (α Sco) - in Scorpio section?
   - Fomalhaut (α PsA)
   - Vega (α Lyr)
   - Deneb (α Cyg)
   - Altair (α Aql)
   
   Medieval Arabic names to test:
   - al-Dabaran, Qalb al-Asad, al-Simak, Qalb al-Aqrab, etc.

4. MONTH/SEASON NAME ANALYSIS
   - Look for 12 repeated patterns that could be months
   - Compare with Latin month names (Januarius, Februarius, etc.)
   - Compare with zodiac sign names

5. CIRCULAR TEXT STRUCTURE
   - Analyze the radiating text patterns
   - Determine reading direction (clockwise? outward?)
   - Look for structural patterns (e.g., every 30° = new section)
