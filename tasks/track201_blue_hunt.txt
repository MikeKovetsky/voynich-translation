# Task 201: The "Blue" Hunt

## Goal
Identify the "Cooling" or "Benefic" plants by tracing the vocabulary associated with `os` (Star/Blue) and `oteos` (The Tree/Star?) in the Herbal Section.

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Herbal Pages)
- `results/dictionary/dictionary.json`

## Process
1.  **Search:** Find all Herbal pages where `os` or `oteos` appears as a **Label** or high-frequency term.
2.  **Visual Analysis:**
    - Check the illustrations on these pages.
    - Do they have "Blue" flowers? Or "Round" leaves (Water/Moon nature)?
    - Are they "Venus" plants (e.g., Lily, Violet)?
3.  **Correlation:**
    - Do these pages link back to the Taurus/Libra (Venus) Zodiac sections?

## Output
- `results/blue_plants.json`: List of candidate "Blue/Cool" plants.
- `results/track-201-results_summary.md`: Summary.
