# Task 204: Quire 20 Master Translation

## Goal
Produce a comprehensive, readable translation of the entire Recipe Section (Quire 20, f103r-f116v) to demonstrate the validity of our Dictionary and Grammar.

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Quire 20)
- `results/dictionary/dictionary.json` (v9.3)

## Process
1.  **Batch Process:** Iterate through every page in Quire 20.
2.  **Grammar Application:**
    - `qok-ed-y` -> "Mix!" (Imperative).
    - `ol-[Noun]` -> "The [Noun]".
    - `ot-[Noun]` -> "From [Noun]".
    - `ok-[Noun]` -> "With [Noun]".
3.  **Semantic Substitution:**
    - Replace `choly` -> "[Mars Plant]".
    - Replace `ald` -> "[Thistle]".
    - Replace `os` -> "[Blue Star]".
    - Replace `ordaiin` -> "[Golden Extract]".
4.  **Formatting:**
    - Break text into "Recipes" based on paragraph breaks or Star/Bullet markers.

## Output
- `results/quire20_translation_master.md`: The final output.
- `results/track-204-results_summary.md`: Summary.
