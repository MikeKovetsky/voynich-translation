# Track 84: Cohen Sanity Check (High-Frequency Re-evaluation)

## Problem
The word pattern `qok-` / `4oh-` (mapped to "cohen" or "priest") appears 7,411 times (approx 18% of text). 
In natural languages, nouns rarely exceed 1-2%. 
The most common words are usually:
1. Articles (the)
2. Conjunctions (and)
3. Prepositions (of, in)
4. Copula verbs (is)

## Hypothesis
"Cohen" is NOT "priest". It is likely:
1. The definite article ("ha-" in Hebrew, "il/lo/la" in Italian)
2. A conjunction ("ve-" in Hebrew, "e" in Italian)
3. A preposition ("le-" to/for, "di" of)
4. A high-frequency verb ("take", "mix", "is")

## Tasks
1. **Frequency Comparison**: Compare `qok-` / `4oh-` frequency statistics with stop words in Hebrew and Italian corpora.
2. **Positional Analysis**: Where does it appear in the sentence? 
   - Beginning? (Likely "Take" or "And")
   - Before nouns? (Likely Article/Preposition)
   - End? (Likely Verb in SOV)
3. **N-gram Analysis**: What words *always* follow it?
4. **Re-translation**: Test alternate hypotheses (e.g., if `qok` = "the", does the sentence make more sense?).
