Track 232: Recipe Elemental Analysis

Goal: Determine if the "Grammatical Element" (`ol` vs `o`) matches the "Medicinal Property" of the ingredients.

Input:
- `results/dictionary/dictionary.json` (with Plant IDs).
- `results/recipe_structure_v3.json` (Recipe Patterns).
- `results/elemental_page_map.json` (from Task 231).

Steps:
1. Identify "Wet Recipes" (high `ol` usage) and "Dry Recipes" (high `o` usage).
2. Extract the *Ingredients* used in these recipes.
3. Check the dictionary/research for these ingredients:
   - Do "Wet Recipes" use `oteaiin` (Water Lily)?
   - Do "Dry Recipes" use `ald` (Thistle) or `choly` (Nettle)?
4. Output: `results/recipe_humoral_analysis.md`.
5. Summary: `results/track-232-results_summary.md`.
