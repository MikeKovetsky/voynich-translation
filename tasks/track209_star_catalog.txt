# Task 209: Star Catalog

## Goal
Catalog the unique star names in the Astro section to see if they function as a "Numbered List".

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Astro Section)

## Process
1.  **Extract Labels:**
    - Identify short words floating near stars in the illustrations.
2.  **Frequency Count:**
    - Are the labels unique? (Names).
    - Or do they repeat? (Magnitudes/Types).
3.  **Pattern Check:**
    - Do the labels follow a pattern like `o-1`, `o-2`, `o-3`?

## Output
- `results/star_catalog.csv`: List of star labels and their coordinates (page/region).
- `results/track-209-results_summary.md`: Summary.
