TRACK 96: Modifier Analysis (The Plant Hunt)
============================================

GOAL: Identify the "Source Plants" (the words following `qok-`).
This is CRITICAL to solve the "Modifier Black Box".

INPUT:
- `results/full_recipe_translation.json` (to get list of modifiers)
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (to check herbal pages)
- `results/scholarly_master_report.md` (expert IDs)

TASK 1: Extract Top Modifiers
-----------------------------
Identify the most frequent words that appear in the [SOURCE] slot (after `qok-`).
Examples: `chedy`, `shey`, `okaiin`, `oteedy`.

TASK 2: Cross-Reference with Herbal Pages
-----------------------------------------
For each Top Modifier:
1. Does this word appear on any Herbal Page?
2. If yes, is it a label? Or a unique word?
3. What is the EXPERT ID of that plant?

Hypothesis: 
If `chedy` follows `qok-` ("of chedy") AND `chedy` appears heavily on f20r (Centaurea), 
THEN `chedy` = Centaurea.

TASK 3: Distribution Analysis
-----------------------------
Are these modifiers specific?
- If `chedy` appears on MANY different plant pages -> General term (e.g. "herb").
- If `chedy` appears ONLY on one plant page -> Specific Name.

OUTPUT FILES:
- analyze_modifiers.py
- results/modifier_plant_links.json
- results/modifier_report.md (The "Plant Identity" Report)
