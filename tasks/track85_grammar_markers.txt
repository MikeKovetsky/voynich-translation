TRACK 85: Crack the Grammar (Syntax Analysis around Anchors)
============================================================

GOAL: Use the 54 confirmed "Anchor Words" (plants) to figure out Voynich sentence structure.

HYPOTHESIS:
If we know `shtshy` is a noun ("aconitum"), the words immediately before/after it are likely:
- Prepositions ("of", "with", "from")
- Verbs ("take", "mix", "grind")
- Adjectives ("white", "fresh")
- Quantifiers ("one", "handful")

INPUTS:
- results/plant_pair_validation.json (The 54 confirmed anchors)
- data/eva_ivtff.txt (Full text)

LOGIC:
1. Find every occurrence of every Confirmed Plant Name in the Recipe Section (f103-f116).
2. Extract the "frame" around it: `[Word -2] [Word -1] [PLANT] [Word +1] [Word +2]`.
3. Analyze frequencies for each slot:
   - What word appears most often at -1? (Likely "of" or "take")
   - What word appears most often at +1? (Likely "leaves" or "roots" or "and")
4. Look for specific targets:
   - `chetaiin` (Suspected ingredient marker)
   - `ol` / `al` (Articles/Prepositions)
   - `daiin` (Verb/Copula?)

OUTPUTS:
- grammar_markers.py
- results/grammar_analysis.json:
  {
    "preceding_words_stats": {...},
    "following_words_stats": {...},
    "candidate_prepositions": [...],
    "candidate_verbs": [...]
  }
- results/grammar_report.md:
  - Proposed grammatical function for top 10 recurring words.
  - Structural template for recipes (e.g., `[VERB] [QTY] [PLANT]`).
