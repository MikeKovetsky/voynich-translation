# Track 312: Morphological Suffix Analysis
# Status: Ready for Execution
# Priority: High

## Goal
Isolate the "Root Words" by stripping the common suffixes. We suspect `-y`, `-dy`, `-air`, `-al` are grammatical suffixes.

## Requirements
1.  **Input:** `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Raw Text).
2.  **Script:** `research/morphology_engine.py`
    -   Implement a suffix stripper for the top 10 suspected suffixes.
    -   Count the frequency of the "Stems" (Roots) left behind.
    -   **Cross-Reference:** Check if the *same root* appears with *different suffixes* (e.g., `chol`, `chol-y`, `chol-dy`).
    -   **Stemming Ratio:** Calculate how much the vocabulary size shrinks when suffixes are removed. A high shrinkage indicates a highly inflected language.

## Expected Output
1.  `results/morphology_report_v1.md`:
    -   List of verified Roots.
    -   List of verified Suffixes and their statistical frequency.
    -   Categorization of words into "Immutable" (Particles/Nouns?) vs "Mutable" (Verbs?).

## Why?
To build a real dictionary, we need to look up "Roots", not full conjugated words.
