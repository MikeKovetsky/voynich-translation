# Task 193: Full Sentence Translation

## Goal
Test the new Case Grammar by translating complete sentences from the Astro section.

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json`
- `results/dictionary/dictionary.json` (v9.1)

## Process
1.  **Select Sentences:** Pick 10 sentences that contain both a Star (`os`) and a Plant (`chol`/`shos`).
2.  **Apply Grammar:**
    - `ol-` -> The (Subject).
    - `ok-` -> With (Object).
    - `ot-` -> From (Source).
    - `qok-` -> Verb.
3.  **Draft Translation:**
    - "The Star (`ol-os`) makes (`qok-ee`) the Leaf (`ok-chol`) green (`or`)."
4.  **Refine:** Smooth the English output.

## Output
- `results/astro_translations_v1.md`: 10 Translated sentences.
- `results/track-193-results_summary.md`: Summary.
