Track 231: Elemental Page Tagging

Goal: Classify every page in the manuscript as "Wet" (Water/Venus) or "Dry" (Fire/Mars) based on the ratio of `ol` vs `o`.

Input:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Full Corpus).

Steps:
1. Tokenize the entire corpus by Page (Folio).
2. For each page, count:
   - `count_ol`: occurrences of `ol`, `ol-`, `-ol`.
   - `count_o`: occurrences of `o`, `o-`, `-o` (excluding `ol`).
3. Calculate `Wetness_Index = count_ol / (count_ol + count_o)`.
   - High Index (>0.6) = Wet Page.
   - Low Index (<0.4) = Dry Page.
4. Correlation Check:
   - Compare "Wet Pages" to known Zodiac signs.
   - Pisces/Cancer/Scorpio (Water Signs) -> Should be Wet.
   - Aries/Leo/Sagittarius (Fire Signs) -> Should be Dry.
5. Output: `results/elemental_page_map.json` and `results/track-231-results_summary.md`.
