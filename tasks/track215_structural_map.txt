# Task 215: Full Quire Structural Map

## Goal
Create a comprehensive structural map of Quire 20 to visualize the organization of the recipes.

## Input
- `results/recipe_starters.csv`
- `results/star_color_map.csv`

## Process
1.  **Merge Data:**
    - Combine Page, Paragraph Index, Star Color, and Starter Word.
2.  **Visualization:**
    - Create a table or ASCII chart showing the flow.
    - `f103r.1: Red Star - Saiin`
    - `f103r.2: Blue Star - Daiin`
3.  **Analysis:**
    - Are the "Red Recipes" grouped together? Or interleaved?

## Output
- `results/quire20_structure.md`: The map.
- `results/track-215-results_summary.md`: Summary.
