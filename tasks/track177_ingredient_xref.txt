# Task 177: Ingredient Cross-Reference

## Goal
Find matches between the "Plant Names" identified in the Herbal Section and the words used in the Recipe Section. This will link the "Theory" (Herbal) to the "Practice" (Recipes).

## Input
- `results/herbal_taxonomy.json` (List of Plant Names from Task 176)
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Recipe Section: f103r-f116v)

## Process
1.  **Load Plant Names:** Get the list of unique words labeled as `PLANT_NAME` (or high-confidence specific nouns).
2.  **Scan Recipes:** Search for these exact words (or their roots) in the Recipe pages.
3.  **Record Matches:**
    - Match: `[Word]` found in `[Recipe Page/Paragraph]`.
    - Source: `[Herbal Page]` where the word is defined.
4.  **Analyze Frequency:**
    - Are some plants "Base Ingredients" (appear in many recipes)?
    - Are some recipes "Single Plant" preparations?

## Output
- `results/recipe_plant_matches.json`: List of matches {recipe_id, plant_word, source_page}.
- `results/ingredient_frequency.csv`: Most used plants.
- `results/track-177-results_summary.md`: Summary.
