# Track 325: Ingredient Cluster Analysis
# Status: Ready for Execution
# Priority: High

## Goal
Differentiate generic `[ingredient_candidate]` words into specific categories (Liquid vs Solid) based on the verbs they are used with.

## Requirements
1.  **Input:** 
    -   `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Full text).
    -   `results/dictionary/master_dictionary_v18.json`.
2.  **Script:** `research/ingredient_clustering.py`
    -   Identify all words tagged as `ingredient_candidate` or `object`.
    -   Check their collocations with known verbs:
        -   **Liquid Context:** `chedy` (Drink), `saiin` (Wash/Water), `ol` (Liquid).
        -   **Solid Context:** `qokeey` (Cook/Boil), `chdy` (Eat/Chew?), `sal` (Salt/Grind).
    -   **Clustering:**
        -   If Word X appears 80% with Liquid Verbs -> Tag `[LIQUID]`.
        -   If Word Y appears 80% with Solid Verbs -> Tag `[SOLID]`.
    -   **Artifact:** `artifacts/ingredient_clusters.csv`.

## Expected Output
1.  `results/ingredient_cluster_report.md`:
    -   List of "Liquid Ingredients" (Oils, Waters, Syrups).
    -   List of "Solid Ingredients" (Powders, Roots, Salts).
    -   This refines our translation from "Mix Ingredient" to "Mix Oil".

## Why?
"Mix Ingredient with Ingredient" is vague. "Mix Oil with Powder" is a recipe.
