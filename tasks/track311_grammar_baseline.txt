# Track 311: Grammar Baseline Verification
# Status: Ready for Execution
# Priority: Critical

## Goal
Scientifically validate the "Subject-Verb-Object" (SVO) and "Imperative Mode" hypotheses using RAW EVA text, not translations.

## Requirements
1.  **Input:** `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (or the raw transcription file).
2.  **Script:** `research/grammar_validator.py`
    -   Define a list of "Supposed Verbs" (e.g., words ending in `-y`, `-dy`, `qokeey`, `shol`).
    -   Define a list of "Supposed Nouns" (words starting with `o-`, `c-`? or just non-verbs).
    -   **Analysis:** Scan the lines. Calculate the percentage of lines that follow the pattern `[Verb] [Noun]` vs `[Noun] [Verb]`.
    -   **Comparison:** Compare "Recipe" pages (Quire 20) vs "Narrative" pages (Bio/History).

## Expected Output
1.  `results/grammar_baseline_report.md`:
    -   Does the `-y` suffix actually correlate with sentence-initial position in recipes?
    -   Do "Narrative" pages actually show SVO structure?
    -   Statistical confidence scores for these grammatical rules.

## Why?
We claim we know the grammar. We need to prove it with numbers on the raw text.
