================================================================================
TRACK 19: LATIN VERB IDENTIFICATION
================================================================================

GOAL:
Identify Voynich words that correspond to Latin verbs commonly used in
medieval herbal texts. This is a CRITICAL GAP in our analysis.

================================================================================
PROBLEM:
================================================================================

We have found:
- ✅ Nouns: aqua, herba, succus, flos, radix
- ✅ Prepositions: de, ad, in, contra
- ✅ Adjectives: calidus, albus, magnus
- ❌ VERBS: Almost none identified!

Medieval herbals REQUIRE verbs like:
- "valet contra..." (is good against)
- "curat..." (cures)
- "sanat..." (heals)

================================================================================
LATIN VERB TARGETS:
================================================================================

COMMON HERBAL VERBS (with forms to find):

1. VALET (is good/effective)
   - valet, valent (present)
   - valeat (subjunctive)
   
2. CURAT (cures/heals)
   - curat, curant (present)
   - curatur (passive)
   
3. SANAT (heals)
   - sanat, sanant (present)
   - sanatur (passive)
   
4. PRODEST (helps/benefits)
   - prodest (present)
   - prosit (subjunctive)
   
5. PURGAT (cleanses/purges)
   - purgat (present)
   - purgatur (passive)
   
6. SOLVIT (dissolves/releases)
   - solvit (present)
   
7. FACIT (makes/does)
   - facit, faciunt (present)
   
8. HABET (has)
   - habet, habent (present)
   
9. EST (is)
   - est, sunt (present)
   
10. BIBITUR (is drunk)
    - bibitur (passive)
    - bibatur (subjunctive)

================================================================================
CONFIRMED PHONETIC MAP:
================================================================================

o→a  h→r  9→s  k→n  c→c  7→l  m→m  4→qu  1→t  8→d  a→e  e→i  2→b  y→i

================================================================================
TASKS:
================================================================================

1. GENERATE EXPECTED VOYNICH FORMS
   Using phonetic map, predict Voynich spellings for each verb:
   
   Example for "valet":
   - valet → reverse map → ?o7a1 or similar
   - Test if this word exists in corpus
   
   Do this for all 10 target verbs.

2. SEARCH VOYNICH CORPUS
   For each predicted form:
   - Search /voynich_raw.txt for exact match
   - Search for similar forms (±1 character)
   - Count occurrences
   - Note position in text (beginning of line = more likely verb)

3. ANALYZE WORD POSITION
   Latin verbs often appear:
   - After subject noun
   - Before object noun
   - At specific positions in herbal entries (uses section)
   
   Check if candidate verbs appear in expected positions.

4. CONTEXT VALIDATION
   For each candidate verb:
   - Look at surrounding words
   - Does it follow "herba" type words?
   - Does it precede "contra/ad + disease" patterns?

5. PASSIVE VOICE SEARCH
   Medieval herbals often use passive:
   - "bibitur" (is drunk)
   - "coquatur" (let it be cooked)
   - "datur" (is given)
   
   Look for -tur/-atur endings.
