# Task 208: Unit Identification

## Goal
Identify the measurement units (Cup, Drop, Ounce) in the recipes.

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Recipes)
- `results/dosage_candidates.json` (from Task 205)

## Process
1.  **Context Analysis:**
    - Analyze `saiin` (6 occ) and `daiin` (4 occ before `ol`).
    - Do they appear with "Liquid" ingredients? (e.g., `saiin ol aiin` = Cup of Water).
    - Do they appear with "Dry" ingredients? (e.g., `saiin ol chotey` = Cup of Wheat).
2.  **Comparison:**
    - Compare with medieval recipe standard units (Drachm, Ounce, Scruple).
    - Is there a word for "Handful"? (`ar`?).

## Output
- `results/measurement_units.md`: List of proposed units.
- `results/track-208-results_summary.md`: Summary.
