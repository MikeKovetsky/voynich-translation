TRACK 128: Recipe Ingredient Mining
=====================================

GOAL: Identify the specific ingredients used in the recipes by analyzing the "Ingredient Slot".

INPUT:
- `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (Quire 20)
- `results/master_dictionary_v7_3.json`

TASK 1: Pattern Extraction
--------------------------
- Search for the pattern: `daiin` (Take) ... [INGREDIENTS] ... `okeol`/`qokeey` (Boil/Cook).
- Extract words that appear frequently in the [INGREDIENTS] slot.
- Exclude known grammar words (`ol`, `or`, `y`, `al`).

TASK 2: Ingredient Classification
---------------------------------
- Group ingredients:
  - **Parts:** `chol` (Leaf), `char` (Root).
  - **Specifics:** `sar`? `sain`? `tor`?
  - **Quantities:** `shey`? `al`?

TASK 3: Cross-Reference
-----------------------
- Do these specific ingredients appear as labels in the Herbal Section?

OUTPUT FILES:
- `mine_ingredients.py`
- `results/recipe_ingredients_v2.json`
- `results/ingredient_analysis_report.md`
