# Task 179: Bio Narrative Structure

## Goal
Analyze the "Biological" section (f75r-f84v) as a continuous narrative/process description.

## Input
- `results/parsed_text.json` (Bio Section)
- `results/verb_morphology.json`

## Process
1.  **Sentence Segmentation:**
    - Use `dy` and paragraph breaks to split text into sentences.
2.  **Subject Tracking:**
    - Identify the Subject of each sentence (Noun before the Verb?).
    - Does the subject persist? (e.g., Sentence 1: "The Water...", Sentence 2: "It flows...").
3.  **Action Chain:**
    - Map the sequence of verbs (`qok-[Root]-dy`).
    - Is there a logical progression? (e.g., Enter -> Mix -> Leave).
4.  **Terminology Check:**
    - Do "Bio" terms overlap with "Recipe" terms? (Shared processes).

## Output
- `results/bio_narrative_flow.md`: Step-by-step breakdown of the narrative.
- `results/bio_process_graph.json`: Directed graph of the actions.
- `results/track-179-results_summary.md`: Summary.
