## OBJECTIVE
Cross-validate our decoded recipes against known 15th century medical texts.

## BACKGROUND

## TASKS

### 1. Compare to Known Medieval Recipes
Find medieval recipe patterns and compare:
- Trotula (12th c medical text)
- Regimen Sanitatis Salernitanum
- Medieval cardiac remedies
- Jewish medical texts (Sefer Refuot)

### 2. Validate Ingredients
Check if our decoded ingredients appear in medieval pharmacopeia:
- Fig (otaiin) - known cardiac remedy?
- Earth (otar) - earth/clay used medicinally?
- Barley (sar) - medical uses?
- Milk (lchedy) - treatment applications?

### 3. Recipe Structure Validation
Compare our SOV + ingredient list format to:
- Medieval Latin recipes
- Hebrew medical recipes
- Italian vernacular recipes

### 4. Historical Context
Research 15th century Northern Italian Jewish physicians:
- Were they known for cardiac medicine?
- What texts did they produce?
- Any similar scripts found?
