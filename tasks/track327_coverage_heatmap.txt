# Track 327: Coverage Heatmap (Global Audit)
# Status: Ready for Execution
# Priority: High

## Goal
Audit the "80% Readability" claim across the *entire* manuscript, not just the Blind Set. Is Quire 20 (Recipes) actually readable, or did we overfit?

## Requirements
1.  **Input:** 
    -   `data/eva_ivtff.txt` (All pages).
    -   `results/dictionary/master_dictionary_v18.json`.
2.  **Script:** `research/global_coverage_heatmap.py`
    -   Iterate through every page in the manuscript.
    -   Calculate Readability % (Known + Root Matches / Total Words).
    -   **Grouping:** Average by Section (Recipes, Herbal, Bio, Astro, Cosmo).
    -   **Heatmap:** Identify "Dark Zones" (Pages with < 40% coverage).

## Expected Output
1.  `results/global_coverage_report.md`:
    -   Table of Readability by Section.
    -   List of "Dark Zones" (sections we still can't read).
    -   Confirmation: Is the Recipe Section (Quire 20) ready for production translation?

## Why?
We need to know where our translation engine fails before we generate the final book.
