How did I choose the right model for the task? 

I spend 12+ hours a day working with frontier LLMs. 
But creating a scientific reasearch this is a completely new field for.

The main differences:
- we need high intelligence in inferring novel scientific problems.
- we need abnormally huge context windows. The gut feeling says ~10M-20M of tokens can be enough for such a project. This is far beyond the capabilities modern models have. As 
- most of the model are overfit to work with small tasks in natural language text (e.g. giving an advice) and code. The leads to awkward moments when translation coverage is 5%, but my Orchestrator is celebrating the victory spamming 🥂 emojis.
